Divorce in Mexico without presence - Wedding in Mexico

If you have previously registered a marriage in Mexico and want to get a divorce, but do not plan to come to the country for this, we can organize the dissolution of your marriage without your presence and as quickly as possible. This will save you time and eliminate the cost of flights and accommodation in Mexico.

The divorce will be formalized through a judge of appropriate jurisdiction. As a result, you will receive a state-issued divorce certificate (long and short), certified with an apostille stamp.

Important standards:

  • the marriage was registered in Mexico, no matter when or in what state,
  • no minor children,
  • separate regime of property ownership (Separación de Bienes).

Required documentation (scanned copies):

  • mexican marriage certificate,
  • passports of both spouses,
  • Mexican resident card (if available).

Divorce is possible without the presence of either one or both spouses.

The term for official divorce without presence — 10 working days, including apostille of the divorce certificate. Express divorce and purchase of a certificate with an apostille is possible in 5 working days.

Our offer covers all costs, including state fees and judge's fees. There is an additional cost for sending the divorce certificate to your country.
