10 clinics for childbirth in Mexico, Cancun - Childbirth in Mexico

Cancun — it is not only the most famous travel destination on Mexico's Caribbean coast. It is also one of the largest birth tourism centers in all of Latin America.

There are really many clinics for childbirth in Cancun. Everyone knows only a few top clinics, aimed primarily at foreigners. But you need to understand that choosing a clinic — not a key point in preparing for the birth of a child. It is much more important to choose the right gynecologist or even just an experienced midwife.

In Mexico there are no specialized clinics like our maternity hospitals. Giving birth here is allowed in any clinic where there are suitable criteria. And they are in many clinics and hospitals. Mexican women generally approach childbirth much more simply than we do. In most cases, clinics do not employ gynecologists, as well as doctors of many other specialties. When a patient applies, the clinic calls a specialized doctor from an existing list, and the doctor assembles a team of consultants needed in a specific situation. Today a doctor has the opportunity to work in one clinic, which is more impressive, tomorrow — in another, simpler one. Almost all doctors in Mexico accept medical consultations, work on call in several hospitals at once, and travel on private calls. At the same time, the level of a doctor as a professional does not change based on his place of work.

You have the opportunity to separately order a doctor, and the doctor will assemble his team, and separately — clinic. Has the opportunity to entrust the choice of a doctor to the clinic, and the choice of the clinic — doctor. Able to invite a doctor for home birth. One way or another, you first need to find a gynecologist and undergo an examination so that you understand how difficult childbirth can be and what level of medical care you will need.

Separate question — C-section. Attitude towards him in Mexico — completely different than in Russia or Ukraine. We covered the issue in this greatmaterial. In Mexico, a Caesarean section is not considered unusual, much less evil. Technology has long advanced, and Mexican doctors use surgery as a way to avoid complications during childbirth. If before or during childbirth it turns out that there is at least some threat to the health of the mother and baby, the doctor will decide to perform a caesarean section. This is required by medical protocols. This decision will not depend on the level of the clinic you choose. This will be your doctor's decision.

Conventionally, we have divided childbirth clinics in Cancun into two echelons. In the first echelon — «promoted» clinics aimed at foreigners. Sometimes their pricing policy is not much higher than that of others, and the level of consultants also may not differ. But the wards will be larger and newer, the equipment will be the most modern, and the attitude will be more hospitable. You will be given a taste of what high-class medical services are like. After giving birth, people are not kept in any clinic for more than a day, not counting cases of caesarean section.

In general, the issue of pricing for maternity services in clinics — This is a subject for a separate conversation and your preferences. It all depends on the range of services included in the clinic’s price list. Sometimes the prices of first-tier clinics at first glance look even lower than those of others. Then it may turn out that the price offer of a top clinic covers only a basic set of services, while a simpler clinic offers — will be «all inclusive». And if you add additional but required services to the price list of a top clinic, the price rises sharply. Price may or may not include the services of a team of doctors. You have the opportunity to create a set of services yourself, based on your wishes and medical needs.

Moreover, periodically and very often clinics publish special offers when the cost of childbirth services becomes very attractive.

One piece of advice. When you arrive in Mexico, first find a good gynecologist, then visit several clinics. Clinics love it when potential applicants come to them on excursions. It may be that your doctor himself will suggest a clinic known to him, based on your situation. The opinion of other mothers is not always right here — Each one could have a different situation. There are also negative reviews about the most famous clinics, as well as positive ones — about the simplest ones.
