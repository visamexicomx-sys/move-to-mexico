Bringing Your Family to Mexico: Reunification 2026

Mexico supports family reunification: residents and citizens can bring their loved ones. Here is how to get residency for your spouse, children and parents in 2026.

Who you can bring

  • Spouse — through marriage to a resident or Mexican citizen.
  • Children — as dependents of the parents (dependiente económico).
  • Parents — as economic dependents of a resident/citizen.

Residency through family ties

SituationWhat it grants
Marriage to a Mexican citizenResidency, citizenship in 2 years
Marriage to a residentResidency as a dependent
Mexican-born childPermanent residency for parents, faster citizenship
Children/parents of a residentDependent status

Documents

  • Marriage/birth certificates apostilled and translated.
  • Proof of relationship and the main applicant status.
  • Financial proof for dependents (in some cases).

How to apply

  1. The main applicant gets or already holds residency/citizenship.
  2. File for family members at a consulate or through INM.
  3. After entry, complete the canje and get resident cards.
