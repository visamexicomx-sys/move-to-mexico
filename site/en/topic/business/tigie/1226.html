Group 09. Coffee, tea, mate or Paraguayan tea, and spices - Mexico Customs

Mexican General Tax on Imports and Exports. Commodity nomenclature and customs tariff

GROUP 09

COFFEE, TEA, MATE, OR PARAGUAYAN TEA, AND SPICES

Note:

1. Mixtures of products of headings 09.04 — 09.10 are classified as follows:

a) mixtures of two or more products belonging to the same heading are covered in the same heading;

b) mixtures of two or more products belonging to different headings are included in heading 09.10.

Addition of other substances to products of heading 09.04 — 09.10 (or mixtures referred to in points (a) or (b) above) shall not affect their classification, provided that the resulting mixtures retain the essential properties of the goods of these headings. Otherwise, such mixtures are not included in this group. Products which are mixtures of flavoring additives or mixtures of seasonings are contained in heading 21.03.

2. This group does not contain cubeba pepper (Piper cubeba) or other products of heading 12.11.
