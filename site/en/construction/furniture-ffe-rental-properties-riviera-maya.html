Local artisan workshops (best value for custom pieces): Playa del Carmen, Mérida. Liverpool/Palacio de Hierro (department stores): mid-range. IKEA Cancún: budget basics. Custom tropical hardwood furniture from local carpenters: best quality AND value vs imported.

Outdoor: aluminum frame + Sunbrella fabric, or teak. Indoor: tropical hardwood (parota, tzalam), concrete, metal. AVOID: MDF/particle board (swells in humidity), untreated pine (termites in 1–2 years), cheap wicker (mold). Mattresses: get waterproof covers, replace every 3–5 years (humidity accelerates wear).
