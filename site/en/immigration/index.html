Frequently Asked Questions

Main paths: residency by income (~$4,400/mo), by marriage to a Mexican, by a child born in Mexico, by a job offer, or by buying property worth ~$586,000+. After 5 years of residency (2 with family) you can apply for citizenship.

Mexico is one of the most accessible countries for residency: no language required upfront, transparent requirements, and 1–2 month processing. The key is choosing the right consulate and preparing documents correctly.

For most people, residency by financial solvency (proof of income or savings). With a Mexican spouse or child the path is shorter and has no financial requirement.
