Get married in Mexico, quickly and without much hassle — Свадьба в Мексике

Planning a vacation in Mexico and looking to get legally married while enjoying the Caribbean coast? We provide official marriage registration services for foreign couples in Mexico and handle the entire process for you — so you can focus on your trip, not the paperwork.

Location: Cancun, Playa del Carmen, and the Caribbean Coast

The Mexican state of Quintana Roo offers one of the simplest marriage procedures in the country. In most cases, a valid passport is all you need. Unlike many other regions of Mexico, there is no requirement to provide a birth certificate or proof of single status.

Fast and Simple Process

Your marriage can be legally registered in Mexico within just one business day. Once the official marriage certificate is issued, your marriage is legally recognized internationally.

Required Documents: Passports Only

Before arriving in Mexico, simply send us scanned copies of your passports (the first page is sufficient). No additional documents are required.

No birth certificates. No certificates of marital status. We will obtain all necessary registry extracts on your behalf.

Flexible Dates and Locations

You may choose almost any date for your ceremony — including weekends or even the next available day. We will submit the application to the Mexican Civil Registry (Registro Civil), coordinate the date and time with the judge, and manage all administrative formalities.

If desired, the judge can travel to your hotel, villa, beach venue, or other chosen location for the official registration.

We recommend arriving in Mexico at least one day before the ceremony. Under Mexican law, a blood test is required and must be completed before or shortly after the marriage registration. Once the test results are available, the process can proceed without delay.

If needed, we can provide four witnesses for the registration. You are also welcome to bring your own witnesses, although we recommend avoiding last-minute changes.

The official registration at the Civil Registry usually takes about 20 minutes. The marriage certificate is issued immediately afterward. To make the document legally valid for use abroad, an apostille must be added.

Apostille Processing: 3-4 Business Days

If your travel schedule allows, you may wait for the apostille in Mexico. Otherwise, we can arrange DHL delivery of the completed apostilled certificate anywhere in the world.

Although the legal registration itself takes only one day, we generally recommend staying in Mexico for approximately one week to enjoy your vacation and allow sufficient time for apostille processing.

Price: USD 1,500 — All Inclusive

The package includes:

  • All government fees and administrative charges
  • Judge and assistant fees
  • Witness services
  • Blood test service (performed at your hotel)
  • Apostille of the marriage certificate

Not included:

  • Transfers and private transportation (including VIP service)
  • Off-site ceremony arrangements
  • Translation of the marriage certificate
  • Wedding ceremony setup at scenic locations
  • Hair, stylist, and makeup services
  • Wedding attire and accessories rental
  • Photography and video services
  • DHL delivery of the apostilled certificate

Additional services are available upon request for an extra fee.

Our all-inclusive package is competitively priced compared to similar services in Mexico, while maintaining strict adherence to agreed timelines and conditions.

Your personal presence is required only twice: for the blood test at your hotel and for the official registration itself. We handle all paperwork in advance and bring the documents directly to your hotel for signing. Only your passports are required at the ceremony.

We also manage the apostille process and deliver the finalized original marriage certificate directly to your hotel or arrange international courier delivery.

Simply let us know your arrival date in Mexico, and we will take care of the rest.
