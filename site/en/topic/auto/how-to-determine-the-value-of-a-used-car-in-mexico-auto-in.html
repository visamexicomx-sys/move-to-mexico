How to determine the value of a used car in Mexico - Auto in Mexico

One of the first questions that foreigners have after moving to Mexico is — how much a car is actually valued on the local market. Especially when it comes to a used car. You found an ad, the seller names the price, the car looks decent. But it is quite difficult to understand whether this price is adequate. Some people turn to the American Kelley Blue Book because they believe that the valuation methods are the same. However, in Mexican realities, foreign services turn out to be useless. Because the Mexican automobile market has its own subtleties.

Mexico has its own assessment tool — the so-called «Blue Book».Libro Azul— This is an annually updated guide containing market prices of cars that are sold in the country. In fact, it is a standard tool that everyone uses: private buyers, car dealers, banks, insurance companies, etc. Despite the existence of other valuation services, Libro Azul remains the most famous car price guide in Mexico.

Libro Azul covers materials on new and used cars, takes into account different model versions, and in addition provides market statistics. The price is calculated based on data on real transactions in the country's automobile market. For this reason, these values ​​can be considered a guideline for a fair price.

For the buyer of Libro Azul — a way to understand if the price is too high. If you find a car for 240,000 pesos and the reference book shows a range of 210,000 — 230,000, this means that the price may be a little steep and you have some bargaining grounds. If you're selling a car, the guide will help you determine a realistic price. A price that is too high will scare away buyers; a price that is too low — you will receive less than the actual cost. Libro Azul allows you to find a reasonable balance.

Banks and financial companies use this guide to determine the value of a car for auto loans. Insurance companies use Libro Azul data when calculating insurance payments. When a car is totaled, compensation is usually calculated based on its market value rather than the original price.

To determine the cost of a Libro Azul car, several parameters are taken into account. Key ones:

  • car make,
  • model,
  • year of manufacture,
  • model version,
  • body type,
  • engine type,
  • transmission,
  • equipment

In addition, the condition of the car is taken into account.

Excellent condition — this is when the car:

  • no damage
  • has not undergone major repairs,
  • fully operational,
  • has a good service history.

Such cars are priced closer to the upper end of the range.

Good condition — The car is technically sound, but may have:

  • minor cosmetic defects,
  • traces of use,
  • minor scratches.

This is the most common category for used cars.

Satisfactory condition — the car has the right to have:

  • noticeable signs of wear,
  • problems with appearance,
  • need for minor repairs.

The price for this condition is usually below average. The car may not have been serviced for a long time.

But even if Libro Azul shows a certain price, the actual price of a particular car may differ, since in reality it is also influenced by:

  • car mileage: the higher the mileage, the lower the cost,
  • body condition: traces of accidents or serious repairs sharply reduce the price,
  • service history: the presence of a service history increases the price,
  • other options: multimedia system, leather interior, navigation system, parking cameras, popularity of the model.

The system also takes into account the popularity of the model. Such cars sell faster and more expensive. In particular, such models as Nissan Versa, Nissan Sentra, Chevrolet Aveo, Volkswagen Vento are traditionally in demand on the Mexican market.

Let's say, this is how the average prices for Chevrolet Aveo models of different years of production in good condition can differ:

  • 2016 — 125100 pesos
  • 2017 — 131000 pesos
  • 2018 — 164600 pesos

The process of evaluating a car on the Libro Azul website is quite simple.

Step 1: Select your vehicle type. The system will prompt you to choose: new cars or used cars.

Step 2. Specify characteristics. Enter:

  • brand,
  • model,
  • year of manufacture,
  • version.

Step 3: Look at the price range. The system will show the estimated price of the car. In most cases, this is a range rather than one fixed price.

Step 4: Compare the price with your listing. Now you can understand whether the price corresponds to the market, whether it will cost to bargain, whether the deal is profitable.

Basic use of the Libro Azul website is free, however, the free version has limitations. A limited number of free searches are available. If you actively work with cars (buying and selling cars), you may need a paid subscription.

In addition, you can purchase the complete guide in several formats:

  • printed manual: new and used cars — 1695 pesos, old models — 1570 pesos, motorcycles — 1360 pesos,
  • PDF version,
  • computer version,
  • mobile version (application).

Digital versions usually cost 1,295-1,595 pesos.

Currently, the New and Used Car Guide covers models from 2015 to 2026, while the Old Model Guide — models from 1990 to 2014.

The Libro Azul mobile application allows you to quickly check the price of a car directly from your smartphone. The functionality of the application is quite simple. The user has the opportunity:

  • choose a car brand,
  • indicate the model,
  • indicate the year of manufacture,
  • get an estimated price.

The application's interface is minimalistic and clear, which is why even people with basic knowledge of Spanish can use it easily. In addition, the mobile application is faster and constantly updated.

However, the application also has its limitations. For example, there are fewer selection options available in the application, and sometimes you cannot select the car’s condition or other characteristics. The application is officially available only for Android.

Although the Libro Azul reference book — a good guide; in practice, experienced buyers use several sources at the same time. Here are some more ways to check the price of a car.

1. Advertisements on Mercado Libre. This is the largest classifieds platform in the country. Service advantage — a significant number of advertisements. Thousands of offers from private sellers, dealers, and car dealerships are published here. After just a few minutes of viewing, it becomes clear in what range the desired model is sold.

2. Online car sales platforms, say Kavak or Automotive Guide. Such platforms sell cars after technical verification and preparation. Prices there are usually a little higher than those from private sellers, but they give a good idea of ​​the market value of a car in good condition.

3. Advertisements for dealers and car showrooms. Many car dealerships (agencias) sell used cars with a warranty. Such machines are usually more expensive, and their price shows the upper limit of the market. If a private seller is asking for a price comparable to the dealer's, that's a reason to ask other questions.

4. Comparison with similar models. Sometimes it is useful to look at prices not only for the exact model, but also for cars of the same class. Makes it easier to understand whether the price goes beyond market logic.

5. Checking through insurance and financial calculators. Some insurance and financial services publish their own car value estimates.

The main mistake of novice buyers — focus on one ad. In practice, the price of a car is formed differently.

See at least 10-20 ads: average price, lower limit, upper limit of the market. Discard extreme values. Very cheap offers often mean hidden defects, problems with documents, or urgent sales. Ads that are too expensive may simply not sell for a long time.

Consider your mileage. Two identical cars may differ in price by tens of thousands of pesos due to mileage.

Pay attention to the package. In Mexico, the same model has the possibility of having several versions, basic, medium, maximum. The price difference between them sometimes reaches 40-60 thousand pesos.

See how long the ad stays up. If a car has been on the market for several months, it often means it's overpriced. If the ad disappears after a few days, then the price was attractive.

For this reason, the best way to determine the price of a car — combine data from the Libro Azul directory with analysis of real advertisements on the market.
