Wedding ceremony in a cenote on the Riviera Maya in Mexico - Wedding in Mexico

Cenotes — guardians of the ancient spirit of the Mayan people and one of the main attractions of the Caribbean coast of Mexico. After all, in the whole world there are cenotes only in Mexico in the Yucatan. To be in Mexico and not visit cenotes — completely wrong!

The Mayans considered cenotes sacred and held their ceremonies here. Mysterious, cool, filled with crystal clear water, cenotes are still an ideal place for romantic events today. And the wedding ceremony in a cenote — This is just exotic for all exotics!

We offer a wedding ceremony in one of the cenotes on the Mexican Riviera Maya. The decorations are simply unnecessary — The cenote itself is the main decoration. The ceremony will be conducted by a real shaman with his assistants — Mayan warriors. They look the same as their ancestors looked many centuries ago. Guiding the newlyweds with spells and a colorful performance, the shaman will unite the hearts together and fill them with harmony and mutual respect. All the forces of the elements will be aimed at cementing a new union of young hearts. It's just magic!

The shaman's ritual will leave a lasting impression. The colorful outfits of the warriors, their cries and dances, which combine the symbolism of people and animals, surprisingly correspond to the spirit of the cenote and the mystery of the moment. You will tell your friends for a long time that your wedding was performed by a real shaman, and describe this bright show in detail. And how vivid the photographs taken by our professional cameramen will be!

In addition, we will hold a sand ceremony — traditional Caribbean rite of marriage, and the cleansing ceremony of copalem — incense made from the resin of a sacred tropical tree.

The bride and groom will enjoy champagne and parting words from our master of ceremonies. The personal love story of young hearts he tells will perfectly emphasize the mystery of the moment. At the end of the ceremony, our photographers will invite the newlyweds and guests to a photo session.

Total duration of a wedding ceremony in a cenote — 2 hours. It will be an unforgettable two hours!

Price:1650 USD.

  • romantic dinner.

We will clarify all your wishes in advance, agree on the date and time of the ceremony and will always be in touch with you.

In addition, we offer organization of official marriage registration in Mexico, without a ceremony.
