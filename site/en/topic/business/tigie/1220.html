Group 06. Living trees and other plants; bulbs, roots and other similar parts of plants; cut flowers and ornamental foliage - Mexico Customs

Mexican General Tax on Imports and Exports. Commodity nomenclature and customs tariff

SECTION II

PLANT PRODUCTS

Note:

1. In this section, the term «granules» means products agglomerated either directly by compression or with the addition of a binder in an amount not exceeding 3% by weight.

GROUP 06

LIVING TREES AND OTHER PLANTS; BULBS, ROOTS AND OTHER SIMILAR PARTS OF PLANTS; CUT FLOWERS AND ORNAMENTAL GREENS

Notes:

1. Subject to the exceptions specified in the second part of the name of heading 06.01, this chapter contains only living trees and plants (including vegetable seedlings), usually supplied from nurseries or greenhouses for planting or for decorative purposes; However, this group does not include potato tubers, onions, shallots, garlic or other products of group 07.

2. Any reference made in heading 06.03 or 06.04 to goods of any kind is to be construed as a reference to bouquets, flower baskets, wreaths and similar articles made wholly or partly of goods of that kind, excluding accessories of other materials. However, these headings do not contain collages or similar decorative images of heading 97.01.
