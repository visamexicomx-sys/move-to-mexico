Quick registration of marriage in Mexico before moving to the USA - Wedding in Mexico

There is currently a large flow of Ukrainian refugees passing through Mexico on their way to the US border. The United States is temporarily allowing Ukrainians visa-free entry under a so-called humanitarian pass, and about 500 Ukrainians cross the U.S. border from Mexico every day. You can now cross the border in 10 hours or even faster; the queues are much smaller than they were in March. From February 20 to April 11, 9,660 Ukrainians arrived in Mexico, the Ukrainian Ambassador reported. Of course, not all of them headed to Tijuana, to the US border. In total, the United States has announced its readiness to accept 100 thousand refugees from Ukraine.

Russians also try to get to the United States through Mexico, but with less success. The humanitarian password does not apply to them. To submit a petition, they wait their turn at pedestrian crossings, and many, in the old-fashioned way, try to illegally enter through vehicular crossings, posing as Americans.

Young couples decide to seek asylum in the United States at the last minute. We heard about the prospect, packed our bags, and flew to Mexico. The Mexican Migration Service is now quite loyal to the screening of arriving Ukrainians and Russians, and the number of entry refusals has noticeably decreased.

The humanitarian password applies only to Ukrainians. What to do if one of the couple is Ukrainian and the other is Russian? The answer is simple: in order to be allowed into the USA together, you must be a family. And confirm this with a document. Get married in Mexico before moving to the US.

If you want your petition to be considered as one in an American court, you must also be a family. This concerns, first of all, Russians. There are significantly fewer families on the border than single people.
