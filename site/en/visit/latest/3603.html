Available routes to fly to Mexico during the quarantine period - Travel news

During the time of coronavirus, the number of available routes from Europe to Mexico has sharply decreased. During the current period, direct flights are again available from Russia. In addition, from Ukraine, Russia and Belarus, flights to Mexico City or Cancun are possible only through:

  • Istanbul (Turkish Airlines),
  • Frankfurt (Lufthansa),
  • Paris (Air France),
  • Amsterdam (KLM),
  • Madrid,
  • Lisbon,
  • USA — subject to restrictions.

There are other lesser-known routes and airline combinations.

Here we have collected all possible plane routes from Ukraine, Russia and Belarus to Mexico that are currently available. We plan to update this publication as changes occur in the future. If we haven’t indicated a route, it means we don’t consider it relevant.

The situation changes almost daily. Some powers are strengthening quarantine restrictions, while others, on the contrary, are lifting them. We have not indicated all flight details. There may be other specifics, for example, your citizenship or what country you visited shortly before traveling to Mexico. If you require more information on a specific route, ask in the comments below the post. We also publish important news on the main routes in thissection.

In the publication under «CIS» We understand any city in the territory of the countries of the former USSR, from where there are flights to Istanbul, Frankfurt, Paris, Amsterdam, London or the USA.

Important note. If you purchase a ticket through an aggregator website, pay attention to connections. If «docking is indicated» or «independent baggage transfer», study the details of such a connection to avoid surprises.

Direct flights Moscow — Cancun

They are operated by two airlines:

  • Azur Air — 2 times a week,
  • Nordwind — 3 times a month,
  • Aeroflot — 5 times a week.

Rules apply for entry into Mexico, and on the way back — to Russia.

To Mexico:

  • Need a visaor electronic permission.
  • Testnot needed for coronavirus.
  • Vaccination certificatenot needed.
  • Health formcancelled.

To Russia for Russian citizens:

  • Testfor coronavirus is not required upon landing; it is done upon arrival.
  • Health formfilled out on the plane.
  • Questionnaire«Registration of those arriving in the Russian Federation» on the State Services portal is filled out before arrival.

For foreigners when entering Russia, special conditions apply. They are described in other publications on our website.

Via Istanbul

CIS — Istanbul — Mexico City / Cancun

Flight segments and airlines:

  • CIS — Istanbul: any airline. If it's a low-cost airline, you'll have to make your own connections and reload your luggage.
  • Istanbul — Mexico City / Cancun: Turkish Airlines.

OR

  • CIS — Mexico City / Cancun: Turkish Airlines. This is a single ticket; connections are arranged by the airline. This is currently the most popular route to Mexico.

Features of the route: when flying from Istanbul to Cancun, there is a technical stop in Mexico City without leaving the plane; when flying from Mexico City to Istanbul, there is a technical stop in Cancun without leaving the plane.

You do not leave the transit area of ​​the airport in Istanbul:

  • Visanot needed.
  • Testnot needed for coronavirus.
  • Health formfilled out electronically before boarding the flight or on paper on the plane.
  • BaggageThe airline is overloaded. If you are flying to Istanbul on a regular airline, say, UIA or Aeroflot, baggage reloading on Turkish Airlines is also possible.

You are leaving the transit area of ​​Istanbul airport:

  • Visanot needed.
  • Testneeded for coronavirus. The PCR test must be taken no earlier than 72 hours before boarding a flight to Turkey and presented in English or the language of the country of departure.
  • Health formmust be completed electronically no earlier than 72 hours before arrival, if there are more than 24 hours between transfers.
  • Baggageyou are overloading yourself. If you fly to Istanbul with a low-cost airline, you will have to go through passport control to enter Turkey (no visa is needed, but you need a coronavirus test), purchase luggage, check your luggage for the next flight and go through passport control to leave.

In reversethe requirements are similar.

If you do not have residence or citizenship of Mexico or the European Union, you may not be allowed on a direct flight from Russia to the EU, since passenger flights there are not allowed. But you are allowed to fly to the EU via Turkey.

CIS — Istanbul — USA — Mexico

Flight segments and airlines:

  • CIS — Istanbul: any airline. If it's a low-cost airline, you'll have to make your own connections and reload your luggage.
  • Istanbul — USA: Turkish Airlines or any American airline.
  • USA — Mexico: any American or Mexican airline.

OR

  • CIS — USA: Turkish Airlines. This is a single ticket; connections are arranged by the airline.
  • USA — Mexico: any American or Mexican airline.

In the US, Turkish Airlines flies to New York, Miami, Los Angeles, Houston and 5 other airports. From there you can connect to many major airports in Mexico.

See the details of the connection in Istanbul in the description of the first route.

For connections at US airports:

  • VisaUSAneeded, there are no transit areas at airports.
  • Vaccination certificateneeded.
  • Testfor coronavirusneededfor passengers over 2 years old:
    • an antigen test taken no earlier than 72 hours before boarding the flight, OR
    • Real-time PCR test taken no earlier than 72 hours before boarding the flight, OR
    • rapid PCR test (LAMP) taken no earlier than 72 hours before boarding the flight, OR
    • (for passengers who have recovered from illness) a document of recovery + a positive antibody test done within the last 90 days.
  • Health form(passenger tracking) is needed.
  • Baggagereboots on its own. During transit, luggage is checked into the transit belt and not at the check-in counter.

UPD. From December 6, 2021, the expiration date of any test for all passengers reaches 1 day, rather than 3, without exception.

In reversethe requirements are similar.

Via USA

CIS — USA — Mexico

Flights from Russia to the United States may not allow you to board without US residence or citizenship. There are no flights from Belarus.

Flight segments and airlines:

  • CIS — USA: Aeroflot or UIA.
  • USA — Mexico: any American or Mexican airline.

OR

There are quite a few combinations of US and Mexican airports for flights on this route. Aeroflot flies to New York, Washington, Miami and Los Angeles. UIA flies to New York.

    • an antigen test taken no earlier than 72 hours before boarding the flight, OR
    • Real-time PCR test taken no earlier than 72 hours before boarding the flight, OR
    • rapid PCR test (LAMP) taken no earlier than 24 hours before boarding the flight, OR
    • (for passengers who have recovered from illness) a document of recovery + a positive antibody test done within the last 90 days.
