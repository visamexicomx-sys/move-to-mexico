Rules for entering Mexico

When entering Mexico as a tourist, you must have:

  • return ticket— this is an extremely important condition
  • confirmation of the purpose of the trip: tourist voucher, hotel reservation, route around the country, invitation from a Mexican relative, business partner, etc.
  • proof of financial security: cash or bank cards

Check outrulesentry into Mexico for guests of countries.

When entering Mexico you must fill out an immigration cardFMM

This can be done in advance or upon arrival at the airport. The FMM cannot be lost and must be returned upon leaving the country.
