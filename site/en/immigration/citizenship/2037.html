Documents for Mexican citizenship

Sooner or later, any resident of Mexico is able to become a citizen of this country. Every resident has this perspective, regardless of whether he is temporary or permanent. After marriage with a Mexican citizen or the birth of a child in Mexico, it is possible to apply for citizenship after 2 years; in all other cases — 5 years after purchasing the residence.

The process of acquiring citizenship is called naturalization. In general, acquiring citizenship takes from 6 months to a year, including waiting for the opinion of the National Institute of Migration and acquiring a letter of naturalization. To begin the procedure, you need to sign up and submit a package of documentation to one of the offices of the Mexican Ministry of Foreign Affairs (SRE), pass mandatory exams on the history and culture of Mexico, knowledge of the Spanish language, and undergo a short interview.
