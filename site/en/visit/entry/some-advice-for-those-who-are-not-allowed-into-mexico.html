Some advice for those who are not allowed into Mexico - Migration Rules

If you are denied entry to Mexico, don’t worry. This happens. Decided to get to Mexico anyway — take a second chance. And we will give some useful tips.

Denial of entry — This is not deportation. They will not put any stamp in your passport, nor in any «black» the lists were not included. There is no deadline for when the period from which you can come to Mexico again expires. You can try to move in again the next day. There are many examples that re-entry is in most cases more successful than the first. Moreover, there is elementary logic here. Most passengers arriving in Mexico are given only a cursory screening. When you re-enter, you may be one of them.

If you know the reason why it failed, work on the mistakes before the second attempt. Prepare the missing documentation — confirmations, and it is better to have them printed. Don’t listen to those who weren’t asked for anything upon entry, and don’t hope that they won’t ask you either. Everything should confirm the purpose of your visit to Mexico. And the best goal for hassle-free entry — this is tourism.

Read our publication about basic regulations when entering Mexico. In fact, fulfilling these requirements does not guarantee trouble-free entry. But if you do not fulfill one of the requirements, you yourself will be to blame for receiving the order.

Do not re-enter with the same electronic permit. Xfrom it is valid for 30 days, make a new one. Fortunately, you can get a new electronic permit on the Internet yourself, without paying, and in just 5 minutes. At the same time, you can make sure that you do not have any ban on entry to Mexico.

Instead of an electronic permit, get a Mexico visa. This advice from applies to those whose place of birth is — republics of Transcaucasia and Central Asia. Even though there is no formal ban on the entry of citizens of the Russian Federation with their place of birth in these states, we all know that these Russians most often have a second passport — powers of birth. The attitude of the Mexico Migration Service towards the entry of citizens of «CIS countries» It is well known that they are denied entry very often. And it doesn’t matter that a person was born in Central Asia during the USSR.

Entry with a Mexico visa is preferable to entry with an E-Permit or other visas. It is assumed that when you receive a Mexico visa, you have already been checked by the consulate.

Confirmation of financial solvency has recently become one of the most pressing issues both when obtaining a Mexican visa and when entering the country. Bank account statements are often falsified. This does not mean that you should dump a ton of cash in front of the immigration officer. Make sure that your statement inspires confidence and that your financial solvency is not in doubt. Support the extract with a certificate from your place of employment, this is not necessary.

Change your arrival airport. We're not sure this is good advice because we can't back it up with statistics. But in Cancún the number of refusals at entry to tourists is really less than in Mexico City. There are more fewer flights at smaller airports, such as Guadalajara, Puerto Vallarta or Merida. But there are practically no flights there from Europe. But you can fly there from neighboring countries.

Change airline. In particular, Turkish Airlines has the ability to not allow you onto a flight if you have previously flown to Mexico with this airline and were denied entry, as a result of which the airline was forced to take you back. In other words, the airline will sell you a ticket, but will not board you on the flight.

Do not buy tickets to and from Mexico in one booking. When you receive a ticket at the entrance, you are no longer entitled to return your return ticket, since you have already used the outbound flight segment. This is not always possible, since, for example, Turkish Airlines will not put you on a flight if the round trip tickets are in different bookings.

Fly to Mexico from any neighboring country. The statement that for successful entry you should fly to Mexico from the «correct» states such as the USA, European powers or your nationality is incorrect. If you are denied entry, then you are taken to the country where you arrived, and not to the country of your citizenship. The fare for tickets to Mexico from Latin American countries is significantly lower than from Europe. When you try to enter again, you can save on long-haul flights.

Don’t even think about offering a bribe to the migration service employees at the airport! You will instantly receive a refusal at the entrance. Only this from order will already be formalized as deportation, with inclusion in the migration registers and a ban on entry to Mexico for years.
