Do I need a return ticket when flying to Mexico? — Migration Rules

Do I need a return ticket when flying to Mexico? Yes, it is necessary, it is definitely necessary. «I was flying, nothing was asked of me» — the most useless and even harmful answer to this question. Since the question about a return ticket continues to be heard in various forums about Mexico, we have decided to once again cover it in our publications.

So is it necessary or not? When entering Mexico, a return ticket is required! Moreover, it is mandatory to have it. This is a requirement of migration legislation. Exceptions: you — a resident of Mexico or a holder of certain types of visa, that is, you have resident status in the country or are about to acquire it, or have come to adopt. No other exceptions are provided.

Always have your return ticket, and indeed all confirmations printed out, and not just on your smartphone. This is not a requirement, but a recommendation.

«No return ticket needed. I was flying, nothing was asked of me. Not asked — this is a special case. They won’t ask you, but they will ask someone else. If the requirement is specified in the migration rules, it is applied, albeit selectively. People ask for a return ticket more often than not, and even more often than many people think.

What happens if I don't have a return ticket? In most cases, you will be denied boarding your flight or entering Mexico.

Many airlines check your return ticket before boarding your flight to Mexico. For example, Turkish Airlines will never board a plane to Mexico in Istanbul without a return ticket. The airline, acting as a migration agent, is responsible for the passenger who is not traveling to Mexico, and therefore has the right to check compliance with the rules of entry into the country.

When going through passport control upon arrival to Mexico, you may be asked for a return ticket again. Its absence is fraught with refusal at entry.

A return ticket is not asked for two reasons: the airline employee or migration officer does not see the point in asking you this question, because he does not doubt your travel intentions; materials about your return ticket are in the system if you are flying to and from Mexico within the same booking.

Are there any exceptions based on citizenship? No, no. Americans are asked for a return ticket no less often than everyone else. Another thing is that they can let an American through without a return ticket, they will simply be allowed to stay in Mexico for fewer days. But they can also refuse entry at the entrance. If you are not American or Canadian, entry screening may be more stringent.

«I didn’t take a return ticket because I don’t know how long I will be in Mexico». No one is interested in your explanation. A real tourist must have clear travel plans. Without a return ticket, you risk not getting to Mexico at all or getting a permit for the minimum period of stay. To prevent this from happening, you simply must present all your explanations, but accept or not accept them — at the discretion of the migration service officer.

In addition, the decision on how long you will stay in Mexico is made not by you, but by the migration officer at passport control. And the date indicated on the return ticket today plays a decisive role in his decision on how many days to allow you to stay in Mexico.

«I'm going to Mexico to see my fiance. When we sign, I will stay in the country. I don't need a return ticket. No, I need it. You enter Mexico with the status of a guest of the country ("temporary visitor"), and your intentions do not change this status in any way. But a tourist needs a return ticket. By the way, you cannot obtain confirmation from the registry office with the date of marriage registration without physical presence in Mexico, in other words, you cannot actually confirm your intentions to prepare a marriage in Mexico with any document.

Are you checking your return ticket? They check it randomly if they doubt its reliability. Airlines have the technical ability to check any reservation. At passport control upon arrival, your reservation is usually not checked, but if you are taken for additional checks, be prepared to check your reservation. Fake reservation — this is even worse than her absence.

If you do not want to buy a fully refundable ticket or a ticket with a closed date, which is usually more expensive than usual, use the tips from other publications on our website. Most US and European airlines allow you to change your reservation within 24 hours of booking for a full refund. In addition, there are different booking sites that hold reservations for a longer period of time. It is possible to create a reservation in Photoshop or buy it online without actually making a reservation at all, if you are not afraid of getting caught.

A return ticket can be to any country and for any type of transport, and not just to your homeland or where you flew from. Migration legislation does not impose any requirements for a return ticket. For this reason, both airlines and the migration officer at passport control may interpret the ticket requirements as xfromyat. Turkish Airlines believes that a return ticket should only be for their flights. They can be understood: if a guest of the country is not allowed to go to Mexico, the airline is obliged to return him at his own expense to where he brought him, even if he has a return ticket on a different route. In order for passport control to accept a return ticket to a third country, try to explain to them the travel route. In particular, after Mexico you are going to visit several other countries in Latin America.

Do I also need a return ticket when entering via land border? Formally necessary. In fact, when entering Mexico through a checkpoint at the land border, they never ask for a return ticket.
