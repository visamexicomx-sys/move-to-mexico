Airbnb Rental Investment in the Riviera Maya in 2026

The Riviera Maya is one of the most profitable short-term rental markets in Latin America thanks to year-round tourism. Here is Airbnb investment in 2026.

Yields

Gross short-term rental yields in Playa del Carmen and Tulum are often 6–10% a year, above long-term rentals. Occupancy depends on location, property quality and management.

Entry prices

Studio in Playafrom $120,000

Legal notes

  • In the 50-km coastal zone a foreigner owns through a fideicomiso trust or a Mexican company.
  • Legal due diligence and a notary are mandatory.
  • To rent you need an RFC and to pay income tax (the RESICO regime is favorable).

Rental income taxes

Short-term rental income is subject to ISR and IVA. Platforms (Airbnb) withhold some taxes automatically. It is best to get an RFC and the RESICO regime (1–2.5%). More in taxes for expats.

Management

Many investors use property management companies for 15–25% of income: cleaning, guests, maintenance. This makes the investment passive.
