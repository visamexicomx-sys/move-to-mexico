Mexican General Tax on Imports and Exports. Commodity nomenclature and customs tariff

GROUP 03

FISH AND CRUSTACEANS, MOLLUSCS AND OTHER AQUATIC INVERTEBRATES

Note:

1. This group does not include:

a) mammals of heading 01.06;

b) meat of mammals of heading 01.06 (heading 02.08 or 02.10);

c) fish (including their liver, eggs and milt) or crustaceans, molluscs or other aquatic invertebrates, dead and unfit for consumption or not fit for human consumption by their nature or condition (Group 05); fish meal, fine or coarse, or pellets of fish or crustaceans, molluscs or other aquatic invertebrates, not fit for human consumption (heading 23.01); or

d) sturgeon caviar or sturgeon caviar substitutes made from fish eggs (heading 16.04).

2. In this group the term «granules» means products agglomerated either directly by compression or with the addition of a small amount of binder.
