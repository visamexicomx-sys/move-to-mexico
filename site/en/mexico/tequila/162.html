Tequila producers in Mexico and their brands - Mexican tequila and mezcal

At the beginning of 2021, there were 210 producers in Mexico licensed to produce tequila from 100% blue agave juice, and more than 1,500 brands and trademarks of tequila. All of them are presented in the table below.

The production of tequila is regulated by a set of standards of the state Tip for regulating the tequila market. The production of the drink is strictly permitted in five states of the country: Jalisco, Guanajuato, Michoacan, Nayarit and Tamaulipas. However, real tequila is produced only in Mexico.

Every year Mexico produces more than 350 million liters of tequila and exports 250 million liters. So, 3/4 of the tequila produced in Mexico is exported. The export of premium tequila is growing especially rapidly. The main export markets are the USA, Canada, EU countries and Japan. The price of exports exceeds $2 billion per year. Mexico has achieved recognition of tequila as a national drink with a protected geographical indication, and the global tequila market consistently shows one of the highest annual growth rates.
