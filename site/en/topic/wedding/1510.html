Marriage Registration in Mexico City (CDMX) – Wedding in Mexico

We would like to offer our support in organizing the official registration of marriage in Mexico City (CDMX). Original birth certificates are not required. We will take care of all organizational issues, and you will only have to come to the registration itself. For registration, you can choose a practical day, time and place, and as a result, without unnecessary hassle, you will receive a state-issued certificate certified by an apostille, which will confirm your marriage in any country in the world.

Official marriage in the capital of Mexico is possible for any citizenship, gender, social group (LGBT), HIV status, etc.

We can register a marriage in Mexico City without the original birth certificate or if it does not have an apostille stamp.

Service package price«all inclusive»for marriage registration in Mexico City:1600 USD. The following costs are included:

  • translation of documentation into Spanish and certification of the translation by an official translator,
  • obtaining all necessary certificates and extracts from registers (for Mexican citizens),
  • providing a home address in Mexico City,
  • filling out forms and submitting documents to the registro civil office (registry office), agreeing on the date and time of registration,
  • registration support,
  • municipal fees for marriage registration,
  • judge's fee for registration outside the registry office,
  • prompt affixing of an apostille stamp on a marriage certificate.

Services that you can order separately are not included:

  • registration without the original birth certificate or without an apostille on the certificate,
  • legal approval and signing of the marriage contract,
  • transfer to the registration location,
  • solemn wedding ceremony,
  • stylist and makeup artist services, rental of wedding paraphernalia and outfits,
  • video shooting and photo session,
  • translation of the marriage certificate into Russian or English by an official translator,
  • courier delivery of a marriage certificate with an apostille to other cities in Mexico or abroad,
  • any other wishes.

To get started, send us scanned copies of the newlyweds' passports and birth certificates and tell us the desired date. Next, you just have to come to registration with the originals, and then meet again to pick up the apostilled marriage certificate. In some cases, same-day registration is possible.

You choose the registration location. This could be a state registry office, a wedding salon, a judge's office, or any other location you desire.

Quickly apostille a marriage certificate in Mexico City — this is a problem. First, the marriage record must be entered into the local civil registry. This takes from a week to a month. Only after the entry has appeared in the electronic register is an apostille issued. Our services include prompt affixing of an apostille on a marriage certificate — in just 1-2 days.

Registration of marriage in Mexico City will require preparation on your part. Birth certificates certified with an apostille stamp are required. If you do not have a birth certificate, you should obtain it from the registry office in your home country, it is advisable to certify it with an apostille stamp, scan it and send it to us. In addition, check whether the names on the birth certificate and in your passport match. With our support it is possible to register a marriage in Mexico Citywithout original birth certificateorwithout apostille stamp, orif the names in the certificate and in the passport do not match.
