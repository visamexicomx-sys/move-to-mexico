How to Open a Company in Mexico: Step by Step 2026

A foreigner can own 100% of a Mexican company with no Mexican partner. Here is how to open a company step by step in 2026.

Which form to choose

FormBest for
S.A. de C.V.Stock corporation — for larger business and investors
S. de R.L. de C.V.LLC — simpler and more popular for small/medium business

Step-by-step registration

  1. Name: get company name authorization from the SE (Secretaría de Economía).
  2. Charter: a notary prepares and certifies the incorporation act (acta constitutiva).
  3. Registration: entry into the Public Registry of Commerce (RPC).
  4. RFC: get the company tax ID from SAT and an e.firma digital signature.
  5. Bank: open a corporate account.
  6. Registrations: when hiring staff, IMSS and others.

Cost and timeline

Registering a company usually takes 2–4 weeks. Notary, registry and handling costs run about $1,500–$2,500. No minimum capital is formally required for an S. de R.L.

What the founder needs

  • A passport and, ideally, residency or an RFC.
  • A company address in Mexico.
  • An e.firma digital signature for the tax authority.

Company taxes

Corporate income tax (ISR) is 30%, VAT (IVA) 16%. Small business and individuals can use the RESICO regime. More in our business and taxes section.
