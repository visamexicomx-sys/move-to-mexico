Quick marriage registration in Cancun in just 1 day – Wedding in Mexico

We offer quick marriage registration in Cancun, Mexico — in just 1 day. Express registration is possible for both foreigners and Mexicans and mixed couples. You go to the judge, sign the papers and immediately receive an official marriage certificate. Everything is very simple and very fast.

No need:

  • wait for the registration date,
  • receive extracts from the register of debtors in advance,
  • take blood tests,
  • have your passport translated by an official translator,
  • provide a birth certificate and a certificate of absence of another marriage,
  • bring your witnesses.

You just need to agree in advance with the judge on the date and time of registration and send scans of your passports to fill out the forms.

Price for express marriage registration:1900 USD, all inclusive.

Tell us the date of your arrival in Mexico and send copies of your passports — this is enough to organize registration. We strictly adhere to the agreed terms and conditions!

In addition, we can certify your marriage certificate with an apostille stamp for use abroad. Apostille is included in the price of the service. Apostille will take 3 business days, and there is no way we can speed up this process. But we can send the apostilled certificate by courier within Mexico or abroad.
