Group 02. Meat and edible meat by-products - Mexican Customs

Mexican General Tax on Imports and Exports. Commodity nomenclature and customs tariff

GROUP 02

MEAT AND FOOD MEAT BY-PRODUCTS

Note:

1. This group does not include:

a) products of headings 02.01 — 02.08 or 02.10, unsuitable or unsuitable for human consumption;

b) intestines, bladders or stomachs of animals (heading 05.04) or blood of animals (heading 05.11 or 30.02); or

c) animal fat, except for products of heading 02.09 (group 15).
