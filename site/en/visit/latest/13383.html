Requirements for transit through major airports on routes to Mexico - Travel Updates

During the coronavirus epidemic, many states have introduced special requirements for transit through their airports. As a rule, they relate to the need to have a coronavirus test and fill out special forms. It is important to know these criteria, because without fulfilling them you will not be allowed on the plane. In this publication, for your convenience, we have collected the conditions for transit through those airports that serve flights on the way from Ukraine, Russia and the CIS countries to Mexico.

The coronavirus situation is developing very dynamically, powers are introducing new criteria and changing existing ones, therefore we intend to keep the data on this page up to date.

By transit we mean a connection in which the passenger between flights does not leave the «clean» (transit) area of ​​the airport. As a rule, countries' transit regulations limit stay in this zone to 24 hours.

We do not consider visa criteria here. They are described in other publications on our website and did not change during the epidemic, except for entry bans.

We have collected all currently available routes on the way to Mexico in thispublications

Istanbul

No test needed.

No health form required.

Amsterdam

Testnot needed (from 06/07/2021).

Test language: English, German, French, Italian, Spanish, Portuguese or Dutch. In original, paper or electronic form.

A vaccination certificate is not accepted.

Form. A Traveler public health declaration is only required when departing from «very high risk» (high-risk country). You can view the list of such countriesHere. Must be completed before boarding the flight. In addition, it can be filled out electronically before boarding at the airline counter. In some cases it is necessarydeclaration of quarantineeven during transit (see other publications on our website).

Paris

No test required except for those arriving from17 countriesworld (Mexico, Ukraine, Russia and most countries do not belong to them, but Türkiye does).

A health form is not required, except for those arriving from 17 countries.

Frankfurt

Testnot needed (from 05/21/2021).

Formhealth is not needed.

USA

Certificatevaccination is required.

Testcompulsory for passengers aged 2 years and older.

  • antigen test taken no earlier than 72 hours before boarding the flight, OR
  • Real-time PCR test taken no earlier than 72 hours before boarding the flight, OR
  • rapid molecular test such as LAMP, NAAT, taken no earlier than 72 hours before boarding the flight, OR
  • (for passengers who have recovered from illness) a document of recovery + a positive antibody test done within the last 90 days.

Test language: English or whatever the airline accepts. In original, paper or electronic form.

UPD. From December 6, 2021, the test validity period for all passengers is 1 day, not 3, without exception.

Formhealth (passenger tracking) is mandatory. It must be filled out before boarding the flight.

Madrid

No test needed.

No health form required.

Lisbon

Testnot needed.

A vaccination certificate is not accepted.

FormPassenger tracking is filled in at this address (link no longer available) after check-in for the flight. In addition, the form can be completed on paper on board the aircraft. Presents upon arrival in Portugal.

Zurich

No test needed.

No health form required.

Panama

No test needed.

No health form required.

Cuba

There are no connecting flights through Cuba.

Let us also remind you that you do not need a coronavirus test to enter Mexico, but you must fill out a special formformhealth, both upon arrival and upon departure from the country.
