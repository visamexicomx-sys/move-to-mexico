Tariffs for Mexican immigration services in 2026

We present the tariffs (state duties) for procedures carried out by the Mexican Migration Service in 2026. Rates are the same for adults and children of any age and apply individually, not per family.

In most cases, the amount of state duties is calculated as follows:

  • tariff for conducting migration processes PLUS
  • tariff for a resident card depending on the validity period of the card.

If you have violated the migration regime (exceeded the permitted period of stay or delayed the renewal of your resident card), an additional fine will be charged for resolving the migration situation. Fines are also assessed if you have not notified the migration service of a change of residence address, place of work or marital status within 90 days.

In the tables below, rates are shown in Mexican Pesos (MXN). A preferential rate of 50% for issuing a resident card applies in the following cases:

  • for family reunification (marriage or relationship with a citizen or resident of Mexico),
  • by invitation to work in Mexico (including through a work visa),
  • invited by public or private organizations to participate in non-profit activities (for example, cultural or social projects).
