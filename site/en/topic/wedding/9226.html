Marry an American in Mexico - Wedding in Mexico

If you are planning to marry an American, the easiest way to do this is in Mexico. Marriage in Mexico — This is the case when you don’t need to bother with visas and formalities, and at the same time you can do it in just a week. Get married in Mexico — it's a little more complicated than in Las Vegas, but much easier than in most cities in the United States and even more so in Russia.

Americans do not need a visa to enter Mexico and the flight to Cancun is only 4 hours, or even less. Every day, hundreds of flights from various US cities bring American guests to Mexico. Due to the high competition between airlines, it is always possible to find cheap tickets.

In addition, Russians do not need a visa to Mexico, but they do need an electronic permit — You can do it yourself on the Internet for free in 10 minutes. It is possible to fly to Mexico with a transfer in Cuba, Turkey or Europe, and for such flights a transit visa is most often not needed.

To sign, you can fly to Cancun with one passport. To register a marriage on the Caribbean coast of Mexico, you do not need a birth certificate or a certificate stating that you will not get married in another country. Submit the documentation to the local registry office, take a blood test, receive extracts from the registries, and on the appointed day come with 4 witnesses to the painting. Witnesses can be anyone.

Do you want a real wedding — in Cancun you will find many companies that will organize your wedding ceremony right on the shores of the turquoise Caribbean Sea. And much cheaper than in the USA. No wonder many Americans come to Cancun to get married here. Hotels for every taste and budget — This is also a big plus to really relax in Mexico. The region's famous Mexican cuisine and exciting attractions will make your visit to Mexico unforgettable. And they got married and saw the country.

If you plan your marriage registration in advance, it is quite possible to fit it into one week, which you will spend in Mexico. Americans are very busy, and therefore meeting deadlines is extremely important for them. But it’s more optimal to plan a vacation in Cancun for two weeks, so that after the wedding you can slowly pick up your apostilled marriage certificate. Of course, you can ask someone to obtain extracts from the registers in advance, and after registration, put an apostille on the certificate and send it to you. An apostille is important so that a Mexican marriage certificate can be submitted to the civil registry office of your country or to the authorities and consulates of other countries, therefore confirming that you are officially married.

After marrying an American in Mexico, you are unlikely to be able to obtain a spouse visa (CR1) from the US consulate in Mexico, but you will be able to obtain such a visa in another country. Once the U.S. spouse's petition is approved, the U.S. Visa Application Centers forward the case to the U.S. diplomatic mission in the foreign spouse's country of citizenship or residence. Now the US diplomatic mission in Russia has been forced to curtail the issuance of such visas, and immigrant visas are issued to Russians at the US consulate in Warsaw, which is also not at all easy to get to. But you have the opportunity to negotiate with the US consulate in a third country and, after acquiring its approval, transfer the matter of obtaining a CR1 visa to this consulate. The process is long, but quite feasible. In Mexico itself, when obtaining travel and immigrant visas, including CR1, US consulates require Mexican residence.

But sooner or later the difficulties will end, the newlyweds are able to reunite and live in the same country. And marriage registration in Mexico — just one of the first steps towards a long and happy life together. And the big plus is that you don’t need to spend a lot of effort for this step.
