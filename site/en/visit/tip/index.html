Frequently Asked Questions

Tourist regions — the Riviera Maya, Yucatan, central Mexico City — are safe with normal caution. Avoid flashing valuables, use official taxis, and don’t wander unfamiliar areas at night.

The main currency is the Mexican peso (MXN). US dollars are accepted in tourist zones but at poor rates. It’s best to pay in pesos by card or withdraw cash from bank ATMs.

Restaurants expect 10–15%, porters and housekeepers $1–$2, and guides and drivers at your discretion. Tips (propina) are an important part of service-sector income.

In cities use Uber (cheaper and safer than street taxis); between cities the comfortable ADO buses; across the Yucatan the Maya Train and car rental. Cancun has buses and colectivos.
