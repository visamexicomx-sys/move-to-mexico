Group 07. Vegetables and certain edible roots and tubers - Mexico Customs

Mexican General Tax on Imports and Exports. Commodity nomenclature and customs tariff

GROUP 07

VEGETABLES AND SOME EDIBLE ROOTS AND TUBERS

Note:

1. This group does not contain feed products of heading 12.14.

2. In headings 07.09 — 07.12 term «vegetables» also applies to edible mushrooms, truffles, black olives, capers, zucchini, pumpkins, eggplants, sweet corn (Zea mays var. saccharata), fruits of the familyCapsicumor kindPimenta, fennel, parsley, marjoram, tarragon, watercress and marjoram (Majorana hortensisorOriganum majorana).

3. Heading 07.12 includes dried vegetables of all types of heading 07.01 — 07.11, except:

a) dried leguminous vegetables, shelled (heading 07.13);

b) sweet corn in the form specified in headings 11.02 — 11.04;

c) fine and coarse potato flour, powder, flakes, granules (commodity item 11.05);

d) fine and coarse flour and powder from dried legumes of heading 07.13 (heading 11.06).

4. This group does not contain dried, crushed or ground fruits of the genusCapsicumor kindPimenta(commodity item 09.04).
