Wedding package “official registration + ceremony” in Mexico – Wedding in Mexico

If you are traveling to Mexico to get married here, we want to offer a combined wedding package that covers both the official marriage registration and the wedding ceremony. And all this in one day and in one place!

The ceremony will take place in Cancun in a private villa located on the very shore of the Caribbean Sea, in a private setting. The villa has a shady lawn surrounded by tropical vegetation, ideal for special occasions. A path strewn with rose petals will lead the newlyweds to the place where their marriage is registered. Here we will place a table, chairs and a stylish arch decorated with fresh flowers, textiles and wedding paraphernalia.

A judge will arrive at the villa to officially register the marriage. To the sounds of Latin rhythms, the judge will make a speech, the newlyweds will confirm their desire to get married and leave their fingerprints on the marriage certificate.

Our Russian-speaking master of ceremonies will also give a ceremonial speech. He will tell the love story of the bride and groom, which will not leave anyone indifferent. Then we will hold a sand ceremony — symbolic betrothal in Caribbean traditions, an indispensable attribute of Mexican weddings. At the end of the ceremony, the newlyweds and guests will enjoy chilled champagne, fruit snacks and congratulations.

Our photographers will capture the brightest moments of the wedding ceremony, and then continue the photo session on the beach.

Total duration of the ceremony and photo shoot — about 2 hours. Everything will be bright and unforgettable!

Price:3000 USD.

What is included in the wedding package «official registration + ceremony»?

  • the cost of blood tests, laboratory visits to take tests and deliver the results to the judge,
  • extracts from the register of alimony debts for the bride and groom,
  • state fees for marriage registration,
  • visit of the judge and assistant to the location of the newlyweds,
  • fees for 4 witnesses,
  • apostille on a marriage certificate within the usual time limits,
  • delivery of the marriage certificate to the newlyweds at the hotel,
  • renting a location in a private villa,
  • wedding paraphernalia and decorations, champagne, drinks and fruit snacks,
  • photo shoot and photo processing,
  • commemorative certificate,
  • translation services.

The wedding package rate is designed for newlyweds and does not cover the costs of guests. As a more budget-friendly alternative, we can also offer a ceremony on the beautiful beach of the beach club in Cancun in the morning, when it is not too crowded.

Additionally, you have the right to order:

  • legal approval and signing of the marriage contract,
  • transportation to the wedding ceremony venue,
  • wedding dresses, stylist and makeup artist services,
  • other drinks and snacks, wedding cake,
  • live music,
  • professional video shooting,
  • romantic dinner,
  • translation of the certificate and apostille into the required language with certification by an official translator,
  • courier delivery of a marriage certificate with an apostille.

In addition, we are ready to take into account any wishes of the newlyweds.

Write to us and together we will figure out how to make the ceremony the most romantic and unforgettable. We will plan everything together with you.

To begin preparations, we need scanned copies of passport page spreads with photographs and the desired date for the ceremony. As you remember, passports alone are enough to register a marriage in Cancun. We can organize the registration and ceremony very quickly — This is in case you have a spontaneous desire to get married and have no time at all to prepare.
