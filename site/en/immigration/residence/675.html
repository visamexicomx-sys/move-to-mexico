In order for papers issued in Mexico to gain legal force in another state, they must be affixed with a special stamp — apostille. This document can then be translated in your home country and the translation certified by a notary. Without an apostille, Mexican documentation abroad is invalid.

Documentation issued by federal authorities of Mexico (Government of Mexico, ISSSTE, IMSS, Politécnico, UAM, UNAM, etc.) can be apostilled only in Mexico City, and documentation issued by state and municipal authorities (birth certificate, marriage certificate, extracts from registers, real estate ownership, notarized powers of attorney, etc.) — only from the government of the state where they were issued, or from authorities authorized by it. Documentation issued by the Ministry of Public Education (SEP) is certified only by this Ministry.
