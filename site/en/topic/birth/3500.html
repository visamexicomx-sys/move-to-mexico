How to obtain a passport for a child - a citizen of Mexico - Childbirth in Mexico

If your child was born in Mexico or naturalized in this country, he or she will need a passport to travel abroad. Every citizen of Mexico, even children, must have a foreign passport when traveling outside the country. A passport for a child can be purchased quite easily and quickly.

The passport is issued at one of the 45 offices (passport offices) of the Mexican Ministry of Foreign Affairs (SRE). 11 offices are located in Mexico City and the Federal District, and 34 — in other states, their contacts can be foundHere. In addition, it is possible to obtain a passport for a child at any Mexican consulate abroad.

First, select the office where you will receive your passport, and then sign up for processing. You cannot obtain a passport without a prior appointment. You should only receive your passport in person.

Applying for a passport is allowedonlineor by making an appointment by calling during opening hours of SRE offices. There you can also obtain details for paying for the purchase of a foreign passport. You can also use the same number to make an appointment via WhatsApp, and then you can make an appointment much easier and without waiting at all. We talked about making an appointment via WhatsApp to purchase a passportHere.

Personal presence

Personal presence is required:

  • both parents.
  • one of the parents with a notarized power of attorney from the other to purchase a passport for their child. The power of attorney must be issued only in Mexican territory;
  • guardian.

The personal presence of a minor is not necessary.

We described in this article how to purchase a passport for a child without the presence of one of the parents.publications.

Required papers

To confirm a child's citizenship:

  • Original and certified copy of birth certificate issued by Registro Civil in Mexico, or
  • An original and certified copy of a birth certificate issued by a Mexican consulate abroad (if the child was not born in Mexico but immediately became a Mexican citizen), or
  • Original and copy of the naturalization document (if the child was naturalized in Mexico).

How to obtain a birth certificate for a child born in Mexico and its certified copies, readthisarticle.

To confirm your child's identity:

  • Original and copy of a certificate from the treating pediatrician (Constancia pediatrica para el tramite de pasaporte) indicating the child’s age (for preschool children), or
  • Original and copy of a valid school ID with the stamp of the Mexican Ministry of Public Education (SEP) and the signature of the director, or
  • Original and copy of training certificate (for students), and/or
  • Original and copy of the national ID card for people with disabilities, or
  • Original and copy of school certificate issued by SEP.

He must have a recent photograph of this document, and his data must completely match the data in the birth certificate or in the naturalization document.

Typically, pediatricians do not provide a certificate if they have not observed the child for at least several months. In this regard, make sure to obtain such a certificate in advance.

To confirm the degree of relationship and the identities of the parents or guardian:

  • An original and copy of a valid Mexican passport of both parents or guardian, or
  • Original and copy of INE voting document, or
  • Original and copy of professional license, or
  • Original and copy of a Mexican resident card issued by the National Institute of Migration (INM), or
  • Originals and copies of foreign passport and immigration cardFMMissued by the National Institute of Migration (INM) upon entry or downloaded from the Internet after entry (for those staying in Mexico as a tourist).

To confirm payment:

  • Original bank receipt or proof of payment from a bank account digitally stamped by the financial institution.

Price of a passport for a child (2023)

  • Valid for 1 year: 815 MXN
  • Valid for 3 years: MXN 1,585
  • Valid for 6 years: MXN 2,155

Children under 3 years of age have the opportunity to obtain a passport for only 1 or 3 years.

A discount of 50% of the cost of the passport is provided for children with disabilities, which must be confirmed by a certificate issued by a health or social welfare institution, or a certificate for people with disabilities issued by the National Integral Family Development System (DIF).

Deadlines

In normal mode, it is possible to obtain a passport in 6-8 working days, in urgent mode, if the reason for the urgency is justified — the next day. One, the need to urgently fly out of Mexico or the availability of air tickets do not constitute a valid reason for urgency. Rush feenot charged.

What else is important

Sometimes a birth certificate has been received, but information about it has not yet been entered into the civil registry. In this case, it is not possible to obtain a passport.

You can check whether information about the child is included in the civil registry on this page.page. You need authorization with assistanceLlave MX. At the same time, it is worth checking whether all the data has been entered correctly. If the data has not yet been entered, the entry process can be accelerated. If the data is entered, but there are errors in them, they must be corrected, otherwise problems may arise when the child and his parents receive the papers in the future.
