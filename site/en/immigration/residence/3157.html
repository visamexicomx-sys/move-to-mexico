Residence permit in Mexico for 4 years without leaving the country

We offer fast and legal registration of a residence permit in Mexico after the first entry and without the need to leave the country. You will receive a temporary resident card for a maximum period of 4 years. Everything will take you up to 2 months, which is much faster than when receiving a card through a work visa in the usual way (4 months). The resident card already includes a work permit.

It doesn't matter whether you've been to Mexico before. The permitted length of stay is also not important. We will prepare all the required documents, receive assignments for filing papers and accompany you to government offices.

If we compare the cost with obtaining a residence permit through a work visa, which will have to be extended for up to 4 years with confirmation of employment, then the proposed option of obtaining a card for 4 years without leaving Mexico looks more profitable. There is no need to leave the country, no visa interview at the consulate, no language skills required, and no additional costs to renew your resident card each year.

Having received the card, you immediately have the opportunity to leave Mexico and be absent until the end of its validity period, that is, all 4 years. Then you need to return to get a permanent resident card (PRC), and without any conditions. After another year, you can begin the procedure for obtaining Mexican citizenship.

You will have a work permit, you have the right to register with the Tax Administration Service (SAT) as self-employed and start running your own business or find your employer and get a job with him.

At the moment, this is the only legal way to obtain a residence permit for those who came to Mexico for the first time, have no other grounds for acquiring a residence permit and do not want to leave the country. To obtain a residence permit with our support, you only need a passport. Additional documents — marriage or birth certificates with apostilles — will be required by family members who will receive their resident cards as relatives after you receive your card.

Service price:220 000 MXN(pesos), all inclusive.

The process begins in Mexico City, and you will receive your resident card at the immigration office in Playa del Carmen. We take care of organizing the entire process until you purchase a temporary resident card for 4 years. We prepare all the required documentation and accompany the candidate at all stages.

After you, your family members can purchase resident cards upon reunification with you. We also assist family members with the issuance of cards.

This offer is not associated with the Settlement Program.
