Best Neighborhoods in the Riviera Maya for Expats in 2026

Choosing a neighborhood matters as much as choosing a city. On the Riviera Maya each city has its upscale, affordable and family areas. Here are the best neighborhoods for expats in 2026.

Cancun

AreaBest for
Puerto CancunUpscale, marina, safety
Zona HoteleraSeaside, pricey, touristy
The 500 RegionsAffordable, local infrastructure
Residencial CumbresFamilies, new developments

Playa del Carmen

AreaBest for
Centro / 5th AvenueIn the middle of it all, by the sea
PlayacarUpscale, golf, gated
ColosioAffordable, growing area
Coco BeachCalmer, by the sea

Tulum

  • Tulum Pueblo — the center, more affordable and convenient for living.
  • Aldea Zama / La Veleta — new residential complexes, popular with expats.
  • Zona Hotelera — the beach zone, expensive.

How to choose a neighborhood

  • Families: quiet residential areas near schools.
  • Remote workers: the center with coworking and cafes.
  • Budget: the center and seaside cost 30–50% more than residential areas.
  • Always visit the area at different times of day before renting.
