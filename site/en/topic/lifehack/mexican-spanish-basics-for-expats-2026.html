Mexican Spanish Basics for Expats in 2026

A common question for newcomers: do you need Spanish to live in Mexico? The answer is that basic Spanish helps a lot, but you can start without it. Here is language for expats in 2026.

Can you live without Spanish

In tourist and expat zones (Playa del Carmen, Tulum, parts of Cancun, San Miguel, Lake Chapala) many people speak English, and you can manage at the start. But for banks, doctors, government offices and daily life, basic Spanish makes everything noticeably easier.

Features of Mexican Spanish

  • Softer and clearer than Spain Spanish — good for learning.
  • Formal "usted" and informal "tú".
  • Many unique words: ándale, órale, ¿mande?, chido.

How to learn fast

  • Apps: Duolingo, Busuu, Pimsleur — for the basics.
  • Local language schools and tutors — affordable in Mexico.
  • Immersion: a local school for kids, chatting with neighbors.
  • You will still need basic Spanish for citizenship — see citizenship.
