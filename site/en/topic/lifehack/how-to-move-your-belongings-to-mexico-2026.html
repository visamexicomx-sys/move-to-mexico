How to Move Your Belongings to Mexico in 2026

How much to bring and how is one of the practical questions of relocating. In Mexico many things are cheaper to buy locally, but you can import personal items, and residents have the right to a duty-free import of household goods. Here are the 2026 options.

Option 1. Luggage only

The simplest path: bring personal items in suitcases. Airlines let you pay for extra baggage. Good for those traveling light and buying furniture locally.

Option 2. International box shipping

Companies (DHL, FedEx, specialized moving firms) deliver boxes door to door. Convenient for medium volumes. Factor in duties on new goods.

Option 3. Container (for big moves)

A sea container makes sense for furniture and large volumes from the US/Europe. It is expensive and slow (weeks) and needs a customs broker.

Menaje de casa — duty-free import

Menaje de casa is a resident right to import household goods once duty-free (used furniture, appliances, personal items). It is arranged through a Mexican consulate in your departure country with an inventory. It is important to do this correctly and in advance.

What to bring vs buy locally

  • Bring: valuable personal items, electronics, specific medication (with prescription), documents.
  • Buy locally: furniture, appliances, kitchenware — often cheaper than shipping.
