There are many foreigners permanently residing in Mexico's Cancun and the Riviera Maya. Although the main diplomatic missions are traditionally located in the capital of Mexico City, a number of foreign countries maintain their consulates in Cancun. Honorary consulates have limited representative functions and, in most cases, do not deal with visa issues.

Ukraine

Honorary diplomatic mission of Ukraine in Cancún

  • Head: Peter Foyo Koyaso, Honorary Consul
  • Email:,
  • Address: Av. Copan número 47 casi esquina Av. Bacalar, Supermanzana 19 Manzana 1, Municipio Benito Juárez, Cancun. Quintana Roo. C.P. 77505

The Honorary Diplomatic Representation of Ukraine in Cancun does not maintain consular records and does not deal with visa issues.
