Sending Money to Mexico in 2026

Sending money to Mexico and exchanging currency is a key part of expat life. Here are the best ways to send money, the fees and tips for 2026.

Transfer methods

MethodPros / cons
Wise (ex-TransferWise)Best rate, low fees — popular with expats
Bank SWIFTReliable but expensive and slow
Remitly, Western UnionFast for person-to-person transfers
ATM withdrawalConvenient, but watch the fees

Inside Mexico: SPEI and CLABE

Domestic transfers between Mexican banks go through the SPEI system — instant and nearly free. You need the recipient CLABE number (18 digits).

Cash withdrawals

  • Use ATMs of major banks (BBVA, Santander) — safer than street machines.
  • Decline conversion to your card currency (DCC) — take pesos for a better rate.
  • Cards with no foreign fees (some neobanks) save on withdrawals.

Tips

  • For large sums (buying property) use a bank transfer with a notary.
  • Opening a local account makes life easier — see our bank account article.
  • Keep a backup way to access money.

For most expats Wise is cheapest — the best rate and low fees. Bank SWIFT is reliable but pricier and slower. Remitly and Western Union are convenient for fast person-to-person transfers.
