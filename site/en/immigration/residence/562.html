Help with migration documents in Mexico

Those who come to Mexico for the first time face difficulties when contacting migration service offices. Ignorance of the Spanish language, difficulties with registering an appointment (sita), lack of understanding of bureaucratic procedures, and simply the feeling of uncertainty inherent in foreigners in a foreign country should not become an obstacle to resolving migration issues.

You should be confident in the end result! We offer our services in obtaining an appointment, preparing papers and accompanying you to the office of the Mexican Migration Service to register or update your migration status.

What are our functions?

  • consultation on the process and necessary documents,
  • drawing up documentation and filling out forms,
  • obtaining an appointment (sit) to visit the migration service office,
  • accompaniment to the migration service office at all stages.

We will do all the paperwork for you, we will be with you from the first request until you receive your migration document.

Price for preparing documents and organizing visits to migration service offices:300/400 USD, looking around the office. State fees are paid separately.

Personal presence at the migration service office is always mandatory! This means that without your participation we will not be able to submit documentation or obtain a migration document. We provide escorts in Mexico City, Cancun, Playa del Carmen, Merida and Progreso, as well as in several other states.
