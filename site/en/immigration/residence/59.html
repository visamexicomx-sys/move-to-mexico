Get a work visa to Mexico quickly

Typically, the process of acquiring a Mexican resident card through a work visa takes about three months. The most important and longest stage — waiting for the immigration office in Mexico to approve the employer's request to issue the applicant a work visa. This approval is then transferred to the diplomatic mission. The diplomatic mission issues a work visa on the day of the visit, but registration at the consulate may take some time. Then you will have to wait after entering Mexico, since not all migration service offices change the work visa to a resident card on the same day.

We would like to offerexpress registration of a residence permit in Mexico upon invitation to work, when the entire process before issuing a resident card will take about a month. Approval of an employer's request at the migration service in Mexico — 3 working days, exchange of visa for resident card — on the same day, but how much it will take to obtain a visa at the consulate depends on the queue at a particular consular department. In Moscow, for example, there is no queue at the diplomatic mission, you don’t need to sign up, and a visa is issued on the day of your visit. In fact, we can expedite the processing of permits and resident cards at the Mexican Migration Service as quickly as possible, but we cannot expedite registration at a specific diplomatic mission, if any.

You must have a diploma or certificate to confirm your skills; you can ask for it at the consulate. But to have an apostille on this document — no need! If you don’t have the original, you can show the consul a simple copy and explain that you sent the original diploma to your employer in Mexico. We would also like to remind you that knowledge of Spanish or English is very desirable, since it is in one of these languages ​​that the consul will conduct the interview.

Having received a residence permit, you do not need to stay in Mexico permanently, but you must return to the country in order to renew your resident card on time.A temporary resident card on a work visa is always issued for the first time with a validity period of 1 year. There are no exceptions.Then it can be extended for 1, 2 or 3 years, and after 4 years as a temporary resident, you can receive a permanent resident card, which no longer has an expiration date and does not need to be renewed. 5 years after issuing a temporary resident card, you can apply for Mexican citizenship for the first time.
