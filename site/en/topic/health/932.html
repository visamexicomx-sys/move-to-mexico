Suciqroo Medical Center in Cancun, Mexico offers doctors at affordable prices - Medicine in Mexico

We would like to talk about inexpensive but professional medical service in Cancun, Mexico. For 18 years, Superación Ciudadana Quintanarroense (Suciqroo), a civil health association, has been providing specialized medical advice at low cost. Among the specialties they engage in are — oncology, general surgery, traumatology, nephrology, psychiatry, psychology, dermatology, otorhinolaryngology and others. About 450 people a week are treated at Suciqroo. The price of an appointment with any specialist does not exceed 380 pesos (2023), while in private clinics such as PlayaMed or Galenia, the cost of a medical consultation ranges from 800 to 1,500 pesos. Since Suciqroo, as a social project, has been supported by the state government for many years, it hosts a number of consultants who work in parallel in prestigious hospitals in Cancun, so the quality of medical care is at the highest level. They accept 24 — 30 doctors of different specialties.

The Suciqroo consultation module is located at Ruta 5, Miguel Hidalgo Boulevard and is open from Monday to Saturday from 8:00 to 20:00. This is in the Sunday market area (tyangusa) in region 101, if anyone knows. Not a traveler's area at all, but quite a safe area. You should make an appointment in advance by phone / Whatsapp or . It does not matter what status you are in Mexico, traveler or resident — the cost of service is the same as for Mexican citizens.

The association regularly offers special discounts on breast cancer screenings and ultrasounds to detect stomach cancer and kidney stones. The association has its own laboratory and operating room, however, if complex diagnoses occur, doctors give referrals for operations to other clinics and can operate and treat there themselves.

You can find out about the medical services that Suciqroo is opening in Cancun on their page onFacebook. We can say from our own experience that Suciqroo at the stage of diagnosis is an excellent alternative to the state IMSS system and even the Red Cross.
