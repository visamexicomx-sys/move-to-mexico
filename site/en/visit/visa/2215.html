How to choose a consulate for a tourist visa to Mexico - Passport and Visas

Choosing a consulate is very important for successfully obtaining a visitor visa to Mexico. If you contact the wrong consulate, your visa application will be denied. Even if your documentation is accepted, your attitude will be more strict, which increases the likelihood of your visa being denied.

Typically, Mexican consulates, when issuing tourist (visitor non-immigrant) visas, only serve:

  • citizens of those countries that are «attached» to this consulate,
  • citizens of other countries who have a residence permit or work visa in the country where they are applying for a Mexico visa.

In particular, to open a Mexico visa in Moscow you must have one of three documents:

  • resident status in Russia,
  • temporary residence permit (TRP) with registration by the Federal Migration Service at the place of residence,
  • long-term Russian visa.

There are exceptions. If the consular department in a certain country is temporarily unavailable, it can be replaced by a consulate in a neighboring country. In particular, citizens of Kyrgyzstan are served only by the Mexican consulate in Tehran. When it did not work due to the coronavirus quarantine, a visa was allowed to be purchased at the Mexican consulate in Istanbul. However, resident status in Turkey was not required. But the embassy in Ankara always requires citizens of other countries to show their resident status in Turkey.

Before submitting documentation to a consulate in another country, email them. Ask if they will accept your visa documents. Even if someone you know was able to apply for a visa to this diplomatic mission, this does not mean that you can. One way or another, pre-registration for an appointment at the consulates is required. Languages ​​of communication — Spanish, English or the language of the country where the diplomatic mission is located.

Tell the consulate why you are unable to travel to the country where you would normally obtain a visa. For example, there are no flights there. In addition, you may have special reasons for contacting the nearest consular office, such as illness. A diplomatic mission may attend a meeting and accept papers as an exception.

Before traveling to a Mexican consular office in another country, consider an interpreter. Some consulates practice interviews. The questions will be very simple, but if you don’t know the language, it will be difficult to pass. In addition, you should have your visa documents translated into Spanish or English.

Where is the consular section that serves the citizens of your country located?

It can be in your country.

In addition, your country may have an honorary diplomatic mission of Mexico — It does not issue visas, but it can help you prepare documents correctly and speed up the process at the consulate where you sign up to purchase a visa. The Honorary Consular Section of Mexico in Tbilisi works very efficiently.

It has the right to be in a neighboring country. For example, Belarusians can purchase a Mexican visa in Moscow, and Moldovans — in Bucharest.

Citizens of other CIS countries, you should obtain a Mexico visa from one of the consulates in the four countries. Not in any of them, but in a specific one. We wrote about thisHere.

In addition, you can check this completelistMexican consulates with contacts and write to the consular section where you are closest to get to.

There is an opinion that it is difficult to obtain a visa to Mexico. Actually this is not true. If you have chosen the right consulate, prepared your papers and look like a real tourist, you will definitely be given a Mexico travel visa.

In addition, we would like to remind you that to enter Mexico you have the right to use a US, Canadian, UK, Japanese or Schengen visa, and in addition to this, a permanent residence permit in these and a number of other countries.
