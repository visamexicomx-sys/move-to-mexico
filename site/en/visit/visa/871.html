There are many foreigners permanently residing in Mexico's Cancun and the Riviera Maya. Although the main diplomatic missions are traditionally located in the capital of Mexico City, a number of foreign countries maintain their consulates in Cancun. Honorary consulates have limited representative functions and, in most cases, do not deal with visa issues.
