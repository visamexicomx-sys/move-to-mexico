Living in Cancun in 2026

Cancun is the best-known city on the Riviera Maya and the region largest transport hub, with an international airport. Beyond the tourist zone, it is a large city with mature infrastructure. Here is living in Cancun in 2026.

Pros and cons

ProsCons
International airport, direct flightsTourist zone is pricey and noisy

Jobs in tourism and services—

Cost of living

  • Studio rent in the city: $450–$700, near the sea more.
  • Comfortable single lifestyle: $1,400–$2,000/month.
  • Living in residential areas (not the Zona Hotelera) is noticeably cheaper.

Where to live

The Zona Hotelera is the seaside tourist zone and is expensive. For everyday life, people choose residential areas: Puerto Cancun (upscale), the 500 Regions (affordable) and nearby suburbs. These are cheaper with more local infrastructure.

Work and transport

Plenty of work in tourism, hospitality and international companies. Cancun airport is the largest in the southeast, with direct flights to the US, Europe and beyond. The Maya Train connects the city with Playa, Tulum and Merida.
