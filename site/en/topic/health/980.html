Every Monday at Farmacia Similares pharmacies throughout Mexico there is a 25% discount on medicines - Medicine in Mexico

Mexico has many developed pharmacy chains that are present even in most small towns. Farmacia Similares — one of them. The chain operates nearly 6,000 pharmacies in Mexico, Central and South America and, together with other companies owned by owner Víctor González Torres, controls a quarter of Mexico's pharmaceutical industry. One of the company's brands — Doctor Simi, a cheerful mustachioed doctor.

In addition to the fact that Farmacia Similares prices for many drugs are often lower than in other pharmacy chains, every Monday Farmacia Similares pharmacies offer a 25% discount on almost the entire range of medicines. Pharmacies simply put up a small stand at the entrance, announcing that today is a day of discounts.

In addition, Farmacia Similares has recently been offering another regular promotion: from Tuesday to Sunday (in other words, on other days), you can buy 3 medications for the price of 2.

The coordinates of the nearest Farmacia Similares pharmacy can be found in Google. Onwebsitethe company itself, all of its pharmacies are also presented on Google maps.

Pharmacies also operate free consultation centers, which we previouslywrote, and if you know Spanish, you have the right to be consulted about your health status even by phone.
