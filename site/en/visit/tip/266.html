Charter and regular flights from Moscow to Cancun, Mexico: summary information - Travel tips

(from February 28, 2022, Russian airlines do not operate flights to Mexico)

Three airlines fly from Moscow to Cancun, Mexico. They all fly non-stop. We have compiled general information about these airlines' flights to Mexico to help you choose an airline when purchasing your ticket.

Aeroflot

  • Travel Operator: —
  • Airport in Moscow: Sheremetyevo, terminal C
  • Cancun Airport Terminal: 3
  • Flight frequency: 4 times a week, regular flights
  • Flight numbers: SU 0158/0159
  • Flight duration:
    • Moscow — Cancun: 13:10
    • Cancun — Moscow: 11:50
  • Aircraft: Airbus A350-900 — 316 passengers, incl.
    • business class — 28 seats
    • comfort class — 24 places
    • economy class — 264 seats
  • Nutrition:
    • hot meals — 2 times in economy class, 2 times in business class with a basket of snacks in between, with the option to order in advance from the menu
    • soft drinks, tea and coffee
  • Hand luggage:
    • economy and comfort classes — 10 kg 55×40×25 cm
    • business classes — 15 kg 55×40×25 cm
  • Checked baggage:
    • «economy light» — No
    • «economy optimum» and «basic comfort» — 1 x 23 kg
    • «comfort optimum» — 2 x 23 kg
    • «business basic» — 1 x 32 kg
    • «business premium» — 2 x 32 kg
  • Transportation of animals: 1 in the cabin (up to 8 kg with container), 2 in the luggage compartment (up to 50 kg), and also as cargo
  • Tickets:
    • on the airline's website
    • from agents and on aggregator sites
  • Website:Aeroflot

Nordwind

  • Guest state operator: Pegas Touristik
  • Airport in Moscow: Sheremetyevo, terminal D
  • Cancun Airport Terminal: 3
  • Flight frequency: 3 times a month every 10/11 days, year-round
  • Flight numbers: N4355/356
  • Flight duration:
    • Moscow — Cancun: 13:05
    • Cancun — Moscow: 11:30
  • Aircraft: Boeing 777-300ER — 486 passengers, incl.
    • business class — No
    • economy class — 486 seats
  • Nutrition:
    • hot meals — 1 time in economy class, 2 times in business class, with the option to buy additional in advance
    • soft drinks, tea and coffee
  • Hand luggage:
    • economy classes — 5 kg 40×30×20 cm
  • Checked baggage:
    • «light economy» — No
    • «optimum economy» — 1 x 10 kg
    • «premium economy» — 1 x 10 kg
  • Transportation of animals: only in the cabin (up to 8 kg with container)
  • Tickets:
    • on the airline's website
    • as part of tour packages
    • from agents and on aggregator sites
  • Website:Nordwind

Azur Air

  • Travel operator: Anex Tour
  • Airport in Moscow: Vnukovo, terminal A
  • Cancun Airport Terminal: 3
  • Flight frequency:
    • 2 times a week (winter schedule)
  • Flight numbers: ZF 111/112
  • Flight duration:
    • Moscow — Cancun: 13:30
    • Cancun — Moscow: 12:05
  • Aircraft: Boeing 777-300ER — 428 passengers, incl.
    • business class — 42 places
    • economy class — 386 seats
  • Nutrition:
    • hot meals — 1 time in economy class, 2 times in business class, with the opportunity to buy additional in advance
    • hot drink with pastry
    • soft drinks, tea and coffee
  • Hand luggage:
    • economy class — 5 kg 40×30×20 cm
    • business class — 10 kg 55x40x20 cm
  • Checked baggage:
    • economy class — 1 x 10 kg
    • business class — 1 x 20 kg
  • Transportation of animals: in the cabin (up to 8 kg) and in the luggage compartment
  • Tickets:
    • on the airline's website
    • as part of tour packages
    • from agents and on aggregator sites
  • Website:Azur Air
