How to Buy a Used Car in Mexico in 2026

It is often easier for a foreigner to buy a local car than to import one. Here is how to buy a used car in Mexico safely in 2026.

Where to search

  • MercadoLibre, Kavak, Seminuevos — the largest platforms.
  • Official dealers (seminuevos) — pricier but with a warranty.
  • Facebook groups and private listings — cheaper, higher risk.

Checks before buying

  • REPUVE: check the VIN in the public registry — no theft, lien or issues.
  • Documents: factura (invoice), tarjeta de circulación, paid tenencia/refrendo.
  • No fines (multas) or overdue verificación.
  • An inspection by an independent mechanic.

What the buyer needs

  • CURP and proof of address.
  • RFC (in some states) and ID (passport/resident card).

Ownership transfer

  1. Sign a sale contract and get the factura endorsed to you.
  2. Transfer the car to your name at the state transport office (cambio de propietario).
  3. Pay the fees and get new documents/plates if needed.
  4. Get insurance (third-party liability is mandatory).

Taxes and costs

Annual refrendo/tenencia and verificación (in some states) are inexpensive. Liability insurance starts at $150/year. More on cars in our cars in Mexico section.
