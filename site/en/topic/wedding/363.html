Quick marriage registration in Mexico City without a birth certificate or apostille – Wedding in Mexico

In Mexico City, newlyweds are required to provide their birth certificates to register their marriage. A foreign birth certificate must be original and certified in the home country with an apostille stamp. In addition, the certificate must be translated into Spanish by an official translator in Mexico. When registering a marriage, the first and last name on the Mexican marriage certificate is transferred from the translation of the birth certificate, and not from the passport.

For many, the idea of ​​registering a marriage in Mexico comes at the last moment, when there is no time to put an apostille on the birth certificate, or the birth certificate is left in their homeland. Even if you brought the certificate with you to Mexico, it is not valid without an apostille stamp. Another problem may be different surnames on the birth certificate and on the current passport. We help solve all these problems.

Having a birth certificate to register a marriage is mandatory in all states of Mexico except Quintana Roo. Without a birth certificate, you will be denied marriage registration. You can, of course, hop on a plane and fly to Cancun, where a birth certificate is not required. But first, calculate how much it will cost you to fly to Cancun and back and stay there for at least a week. For this reason, we have another offer for you.

Thanks to our help, newlyweds do not need to wait, undergo a medical examination and attend a premarital course for a young family. The original birth certificate or an apostille on it are also not needed. Newlyweds do not need anything at all except to come to the registry office or to the judge and put their signatures and fingerprints on the marriage certificate. We do the rest for you.
