Medical and Dental Tourism in Mexico in 2026

Mexico is a leading medical tourism destination: quality care at prices 50–70% lower than the US. Dentistry is especially popular. Here is medical and dental tourism in 2026.

Why Mexico

  • Savings of 50–70% vs the US and Canada at comparable quality.
  • Modern private clinics, many to US standards.
  • Doctors trained in the US/EU, English-speaking staff.
  • Proximity to the US, short flights.

Approximate prices (dentistry)

ProcedureMexicoUS
Dental implant$800–$1,500$3,000–$5,000
Crown$300–$500$1,000–$2,000
Cleaning$30–$60$150–$300

Popular destinations

  • Los Algodones (Baja) — the "dental capital," hundreds of clinics by the US border.
  • Tijuana and Cancun — dentistry and plastic surgery.
  • Mexico City and Guadalajara — complex surgery, cardiology, oncology.
  • Merida, Playa del Carmen — private hospitals for expats.

Patient tips

  • Check clinic licenses and reviews, request a treatment plan and quote in advance.
  • Plan complex treatment with extra recovery days.
  • For residents, private insurance or IMSS cover a lot — see healthcare for expats.
