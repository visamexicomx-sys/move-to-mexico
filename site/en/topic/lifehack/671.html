How to avoid annoying phone calls in Mexico - How to live in Mexico

In a number of countries around the world, including Mexico, there are public registries of telephone numbers whose owners want to avoid intrusive calls from, say, television advertising or marketing campaigns. Their code name — «Don't call me». TV advertising operators must consult this register before offering products, services or conducting marketing surveys to subscribers.

In Mexico this registry is calledRegistro Público para Evitar Publicidad(REPEP).

Every owner of a telephone in Mexico, cellular and landline, has the opportunity to add his number to this registry. The number should be linked only to a Mexican operator; entering the number of a foreign operator will not work. After this, the number of calls with intrusive advertising should decrease sharply or disappear altogether.

It's done like this.

1. Go to the official registry portalrepeatp.profeco.gob.mx

2. Click on the first buttonRegistra tu número(register your number).

3. In the fieldladaenter 3 digits of the area code in the fieldtelefono— the remaining 7 digits of the number. In the next field —Lada + teléfono— Enter all 10 digits of the phone number without spaces.

In the sectionSectoresselect the types of promotional calls you want to avoid. It's easier to select everything.

Then click on the blue buttonRegistrar. Registration is completed!

4. If you want to make sure that your number is already in the registry, return to the home page and click the buttonConsulta tu registro. Next, enter your phone number.

5. To remove your number from the registry, on the home page, click the buttonCancela tu registroand in addition enter your phone number.

We wish you a calm life without intrusive phone calls!
