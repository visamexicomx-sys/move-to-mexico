About the consultant

Midwives and Doulas, Yoga and Pilates

Yoga teacher for pregnant women and postpartum yoga. Doula, natural midwife. Mom with home birth experience ❤️ I prepare mothers for natural childbirth. I help you recover after. I get rid of diastasis in a maximum of 2 months.

I have been practicing yoga for more than 10 years, teaching it for 7 years, and for the last 2 years I have been a consultant on yoga for pregnant women and yoga after childbirth. You are allowed to study online.
