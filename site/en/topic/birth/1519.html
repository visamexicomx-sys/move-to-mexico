Registro civil — Mexican civil registry and a complete analogue of the usual registry offices. There are registro civil offices in every more or less large locality in Mexico. Their main purpose — registration and issuance of papers on registration of the most important legal events in a person’s life:

  • birth,
  • adoption,
  • marriages and divorces,
  • death,
  • some cadastral issues, including land and property rights,
  • proof of residence.

In addition, the registro civil issues CURP — State resident identification number.

Paradoxically, many non-residents know only one in Cancun, the «central» Registro civil office located near Las Palapas park. It's always crowded and there's always a queue. Meanwhile, in Cancunsixregistro civil offices and four more — outside the city within transport accessibility. These offices may have different sizes, presentation and number of staff. But all these offices perform almost exactly the same functions. The level of English proficiency among the officials working there is also the same, in other words, almost zero, and even in the «central» office. Moreover, you have the opportunity to register your child and receive a birth certificate at any registro civil in Mexico, without reference to your place of residence.

According to the registro civil regulations of the municipality of Benito Juarez, the center of which is Cancun, registering the birth of a child and issuing a birth certificate must take 15 minutes. The Mexicans themselves register their children in those offices where this time is observed.
