How to Get a Driver's License in Mexico in 2026

A Mexican driver's license is handy for living and driving here, and in some states it is very easy to get. Here is how a foreigner gets a license in 2026.

Do you need a Mexican license

Tourists can drive on a foreign or international license. Residents should get a local one — it is easier to show to police and is needed for rentals, insurance and as ID.

Documents

  • Passport and resident card (or, in some states, a passport).
  • CURP.
  • Proof of address (a CFE bill).
  • Sometimes a foreign license and a medical/vision check.

Is an exam required

It depends on the state. In Quintana Roo (Cancun, Playa, Tulum) a theory and practical test is often NOT required — the license is issued on documents the same day. Mexico City and some states have introduced a theory test.

Cost and validity

ValidityApprox. price
3 years~$40–$70
5 years~$70–$110

Step by step

  1. Check your state requirements on the local government site.
  2. Gather documents and pay the fee.
  3. Visit the office (in Quintana Roo often without an appointment), take a test if required.
  4. Receive the license the same day.
