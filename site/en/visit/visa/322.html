Citizens of which countries can visit Mexico without a visa - Passport and visas

Citizens of 96 countries around the world do not need to obtain a visa from this state to visit Mexico. Just grab your passport and come to Mexico! Typically, these are economically highly developed powers, Latin American states, or those with which Mexico has agreements on visa-free travel for citizens.

No visa required for Mexico

Citizens of the following countries are considered completely visa-free entry into Mexico. This rule applies to arrival by any means of transport.

The right to visa-free entry into Mexico applies only to citizenship and not to residency status in the following countries, with the exception of permanent residence in the United States, Canada, United Kingdom, Japan, Switzerland, Schengen countries, Chile, Colombia, Peru.

While you may not need a visa to enter Mexico, a passport is not enough. The immigration officer upon arrival may ask you for supporting documentation:

  • Return ticket. This is a sore subject. If you do not have a return ticket, you may have problems entering Mexico.
  • Confirmation of the purpose of the visit. In most cases, this is a guest country voucher, a hotel reservation or your itinerary around the country. If you came to the country on business, justify the purpose of your visit with a letter from your partner.
  • Evidence of solvency. You can present cash or bank cards.

If you are flying to Mexico with a child but without the other parent, you should offer permission from the absent parent.
