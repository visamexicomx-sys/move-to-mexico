How to Rent Long-Term in Mexico in 2026

A long-term rental is the first step of settling in. Mexico has its own rules: a deposit, a guarantor, a lease in Spanish. Here is how to rent without mistakes in 2026.

Where to search

  • Facebook groups for expats and rentals in a specific city are the most popular channel.
  • Inmuebles24, Vivanuncios are the largest listing sites.
  • Local agents (inmobiliaria) charge a fee but save time.
  • Walking neighborhoods — many places advertise with se renta signs.

What landlords require

RequirementDetails
Depositusually 1 month, sometimes 2
Guarantor (aval)a Mexican property owner as guarantor; often replaced by prepayment
Prepaymentfor foreigners without an aval, 6–12 months up front
Lease (contrato)in Spanish, usually 12 months

Approximate monthly rent

  • Cancun / Playa del Carmen: studio $500–$700, two-bedroom from $800.
  • Merida: studio $350–$500, two-bedroom from $600.
  • Mexico City (Roma, Condesa): one-bedroom from $700.

What to watch for

  • Confirm what is included: water, gas, internet, maintenance (mantenimiento).
  • The electricity bill (CFE) is often in the landlord name — ask for it for banking and address proof.
  • Document the apartment condition at move-in (photos) to get your deposit back.
  • Furnished places cost more but are convenient for a start.
