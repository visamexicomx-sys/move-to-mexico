Frequently Asked Questions

Popular choices are Playa del Carmen and Cancun (beach, expat infrastructure), Merida (safety), Puerto Vallarta and Mexico City (culture, work). It depends on climate, budget and goals.

Tourist and expat regions — the Riviera Maya, Merida, Quintana Roo — are considered safe. As anywhere, common sense and neighborhood choice matter. Merida is one of Latin America’s safest cities.

The Caribbean coast (Cancun, Tulum) is tropical year-round, 25–32°C. The highlands (Mexico City, San Miguel de Allende) enjoy mild spring weather all year. Rainy season is June to October.

Chichen Itza, the Yucatan cenotes, Tulum ruins, Mexico City and Teotihuacan, colonial Guanajuato, Sumidero Canyon and the Riviera Maya beaches are must-sees for any visitor.
