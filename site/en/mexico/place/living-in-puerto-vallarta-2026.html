Living in Puerto Vallarta in 2026

Puerto Vallarta is one of the most beloved expat cities on Mexico Pacific coast: mountains, ocean, a bohemian atmosphere and a large international (and LGBT-friendly) community. Here is living in Puerto Vallarta in 2026.

Pros and cons

ProsCons
Ocean and mountains, scenicHumid, hot summer season
Large, friendly expat communityTouristy in the center
Good healthcare and infrastructureFarther from the US east coast
International airport—

Cost of living

  • Studio rent: $500–$800, a seaside condo more.
  • Comfortable single lifestyle: $1,400–$2,200/month.
  • A retired couple: $2,000–$3,000.

Where to live

Zona Romantica (Emiliano Zapata) is the bohemian seaside center, popular with expats. Marina Vallarta is upscale with yachts. Versalles is a trendy residential area with restaurants. Nuevo Vallarta (in neighboring Nayarit) has new beachfront complexes.

Who it is for

Ideal for retirees, creatives and those who value the mix of ocean, mountains and a developed community. Many English speakers ease the start without Spanish.
