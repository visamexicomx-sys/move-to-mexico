Frequently Asked Questions

Natural materials (wood, stone, chukum), neutral earth tones with tropical accents, indoor-outdoor flow, high ceilings for ventilation, abundant natural light, local artisan pieces, tropical plants. Think: organic luxury — not beach kitsch.

Design consultation: $15,000–$30,000 MXN (2-bed condo). Full design package (plans, material boards, sourcing): $30,000–$80,000. Design + procurement + installation: $80,000–$200,000. Most designers charge 10–15% of furniture/materials budget.

Tropical hardwoods (parota, tzalam, huanacaxtle): naturally moisture and termite resistant. Concrete and stone: indestructible. Stainless steel: for hardware and accents. Outdoor fabrics (Sunbrella): for any exposed seating. AVOID: MDF, particle board, untreated pine, standard upholstery fabric.

Large folding/sliding glass doors (4–6m openings), covered terrace that flows from living room, consistent flooring material inside/outside, ceiling fans on terraces, outdoor kitchen. The Riviera Maya climate allows outdoor living 10+ months/year.

Mérida: best selection of Yucatecan artisan furniture and textiles. Tulum: boutique decor shops (premium prices). Local markets: hammocks, ceramics, textiles at fraction of boutique prices. We have relationships with artisan workshops across the peninsula.
