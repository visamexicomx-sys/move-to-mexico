Registration of same-sex marriages in Mexico - Wedding in Mexico

Mexico fully recognizes same-sex marriage. This is one of the few countries where it is easy to get into and where such a marriage can be concluded quickly and easily. A same-sex marriage registered in Mexico will be recognized by all those countries where such marriages are legal.

It doesn’t matter what your citizenship, gender, age or HIV status is — The conditions for registering marriages in Mexico are the same for everyone. We assist in registering same-sex marriages in Mexico City and Cancun. In Mexico City, to register a marriage you must have a birth certificate with an apostille; in Cancun, only a passport is enough. But in Mexico City it is possible to register a marriage in 1 day and the next day put an apostille on the marriage certificate. In Cancun, it can take a week to register a marriage.

We take care of all the hassle of organizing the registration of a same-sex marriage. There is a lot to do in Mexico, so we want you to spend more time relaxing, communicating with the country and with each other during a short visit to Mexico. The marriage itself and communication with the judge will take place in the most friendly atmosphere, and we want you to remember this day with warmth for the rest of your life.

By following the links you have the right to see detailed rules for registering marriages inCancunandMexico Cityin our packages «all inclusive».

If you have already chosen an approximate registration date, simply contact us. We will tell you about all the details of registration, answer all questions and guide you until you receive your marriage certificate. We work with several judges and carefully plan everything in advance, for this reason our newlyweds never wait or stand in lines.

We guarantee confidentiality. All services are provided according to the contract.

In addition, we offer all kinds of special ceremonies — on the ocean, in a cenote or on a yacht — and we provide a lot of related services, such as preparing and signing a marriage contract, photo and video shooting, a gala dinner or rental of wedding accessories.

To get started, we need scans of your passports and — only for registration in Mexico City — birth certificates. Any registration date is possible, even on a weekend, but it must be agreed upon. We will take into account all your wishes and try to optimize all your costs, even those not related to the registration itself. Then we are waiting for you in Mexico, and everything will be ready for your arrival at registration.
