How to Import a Car to Mexico in 2026

Many people move to Mexico in their own car from the US. Importing a car is doable, but the rules depend on your status and region. Here are the 2026 options.

Option 1: temporary import (TIP)

The TIP (Temporary Import Permit) lets you bring a foreign car for the length of your stay. It's issued online or at the border, requires a deposit ($200–$400 depending on the car's year) refunded when you take it out. A TIP is needed everywhere EXCEPT the border zone (Baja California and a ~20–30 km strip along the border) and Sonora.

Documents for the TIP

  • Passport and tourist card (FMM) or resident card.
  • Title and registration in your name.
  • Driver's license.

Option 2: permanent legalization (customs)

To keep the car in Mexico permanently, you legalize it through a Mexican customs broker (agente aduanal). Not every car qualifies — usually specific model years. Cost and requirements depend on the model and year. The government periodically announces amnesty programs for older US cars.

Key nuances

  • With temporary residency (Residente Temporal) you can keep a foreign car on a TIP. With permanent residency (Permanente) no TIP is issued — you need legalization or a Mexican car.
  • You can't let a Mexican national drive your foreign-plated car.
  • Penalties for an expired TIP are serious, up to confiscation.

Alternative — buy a car in Mexico

Often it's easier to sell your car at home and buy local: no TIP or legalization hassle. A foreigner with a CURP can buy a car in Mexico.
