Wedding ceremony on the beach of a private villa in Mexico – Wedding in Mexico

The beautiful Caribbean beaches of Mexico — one of the most popular places for a wedding ceremony among newlyweds from all over the world. It is difficult to imagine a more romantic place created by nature itself. An endless horizon filled with sun, turquoise ocean, snow-white sand, green palm trees — I couldn't think of better scenery. The first day of a new family's life should be the most unforgettable!

Thousands of wedding ceremonies are held in the Mexican state of Quintana Roo every year. We invite you to join their number and hold a wedding ceremony in a paradise on the ocean.

The ceremony will take place in a private villa right on the Caribbean Sea in a private setting. Surrounded by lush tropical vegetation, the villa has its own tropical garden, perfect for hosting guests. We will install a stylish arch here, decorated with fresh flowers and wedding paraphernalia, and seat all the guests. The main ceremony will also take place here. To the sounds of Latin rhythms, the judge will make a speech, the newlyweds will confirm their desire to get married and sign the marriage certificate.

If the wedding is symbolic, without marriage registration, it will be conducted by our Russian-speaking master of ceremonies. He will tell the love story of the bride and groom, which will not leave anyone indifferent. Then there will be a sand ceremony — symbolic betrothal in Caribbean traditions. At the end of the ceremony, guests will enjoy chilled champagne, fruit snacks and congratulations.

During the entire ceremony, our professional photographers will work to capture the brightest moments of your wedding. The newlyweds will be able to continue the photo shoot right on the beach.

Duration of the ceremony — about 2 hours. Everything will be bright and unforgettable!

Price:1500 USD.

Beach wedding price includes:

  • renting a location in a private villa for a wedding ceremony (minimum 2 hours),
  • wedding decor and floristry, arch and light furniture, bouquet and floral decorations for the bride and groom,
  • champagne, drinks and fruit snacks,
  • services of a Russian-speaking master of ceremonies, individual wedding speech,
  • work of professional photographers (photo report of the ceremony, photo shoot in the garden and on the beach),
  • commemorative certificate,
  • translation services (Russian, Spanish, English).

The price for the wedding ceremony is calculated for newlyweds and does not cover the costs of guests.

Additionally you can order:

  • transport to the ceremony venue, including VIP,
  • rental of the main premises of the villa, rooms for the bride,
  • auxiliary drinks and snacks, wedding cake,
  • professional services of a stylist and makeup artist,
  • rental of outfits for changing your wedding look and photo shoot,
  • live music, classical or mariachi (traditional Mexican musicians in national costumes),
  • professional video shooting (ground or drone),
  • romantic dinner at sunset.

We will tell you about all the intricacies of preparing and holding your holiday, agree on the date and time and will always be in touch with you.

In addition, we offer organization of official marriage registration in Mexico, without a ceremony.
