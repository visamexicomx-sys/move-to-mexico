Group 10. Cereals - Mexican Customs

Mexican General Tax on Imports and Exports. Commodity nomenclature and customs tariff

GROUP 10

CEREALS

Note:

1A. Headings in this chapter should cover only grains, including those not separated from ears or stems.

1B. This group does not include grains that have been hulled or processed in any other way. However, rice, husked, hulled, polished, glazed, parboiled or broken, is included in heading 10.06.

2. Heading 10.05 excludes sweet corn (Chapter 07).

Note to subheadings:

1. The term «durum wheat» means wheat speciesTriticum durumand hybrids obtained as a result of interspecific crossingTriticum durumwith other species having the same number of chromosomes (28).
