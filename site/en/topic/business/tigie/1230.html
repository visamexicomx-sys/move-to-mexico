Group 15. Fats and oils of animal or vegetable origin and products of their breakdown; prepared edible fats; waxes of animal or vegetable origin - Mexican Customs

Mexican General Tax on Imports and Exports. Commodity nomenclature and customs tariff

SECTION III

FATS AND OILS OF ANIMAL OR VEGETABLE ORIGIN AND THEIR PRODUCTS OF CLEANING; READY FOOD FATS; WAXES OF ANIMAL OR PLANT ORIGIN

GROUP 15

FATS AND OILS OF ANIMAL OR VEGETABLE ORIGIN AND THEIR PRODUCTS OF CLEANING; READY FOOD FATS; WAXES OF ANIMAL OR PLANT ORIGIN

Note:

1. This group does not include:

a) pork fat or poultry fat (heading 02.09);

b) cocoa butter, cocoa fat (commodity item 18.04);

c) prepared food products containing more than 15 wt.% of products of heading 04.05 (mainly group 21);

d) cracklings (heading 23.01) or waste specified in headings 23.04-23.06;

e) fatty acids, prepared waxes, medicines, paints, varnishes, soaps, perfumes, cosmetics or toilet preparations, sulfonated oils or other goods of Section VI; or

e) factis obtained from oils (heading 40.02).

2. Heading 15.09 does not cover oils obtained from olives by extraction with a solvent (heading 15.10).

3. Heading 15.18 does not cover fats or oils or their fractions, denatured only in the usual manner, to be classified in the headings corresponding to similar undenatured fats, oils and their fractions.

4. Soap stocks, oil mixture and fat residues, stearic pitch, glycerin pitch and fat residues are included in heading 15.22.

Note to subheadings:

1. In subheadings 1514.11 and 1514.19, the term «rapeseed oil (from rapeseed or colza) with a low content of erucic acid» means a fixed oil containing less than 2% by weight of erucic acid.
