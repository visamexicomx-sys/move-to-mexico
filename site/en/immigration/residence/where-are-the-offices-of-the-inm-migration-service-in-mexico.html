Mexican Migration Service — National Institute of Migration (INM) — is represented by 32 offices located in the capitals of each state and in the Federal District of Mexico City. INM also has offices in many small cities of the state, where there is a high concentration of foreigners. All of them have the ability to carry out immigration status processes in Mexico.

INM offices at international airports, maritime terminals and land border crossings only handle entry and exit processing and do not handle immigration status-related matters.
