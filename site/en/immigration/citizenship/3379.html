Where to Apply for Mexican Citizenship

Table of contents:

  • Mexico City, CDMX
  • Merida, Yucatan state
  • Monterrey, Nuevo Leon state
  • Pachuca de Soto, Hidalgo State
  • Tijuana, Baja California

At the moment, submitting documentation to go through the stages of naturalization, in other words, acquiring Mexican citizenship, is allowed at several offices of the Ministry of Foreign Affairs on the territory of the country. You have the opportunity to contact any of these offices, regardless of where you live. The naturalization process will begin and end at the office where you submit the documents, and you will purchase the naturalization letter there.

Mexico City, CDMX

Address: Dirección General de Asuntos Jurídicos, Plaza Juárez, Avenida Juárez 20, planta baja, Colonia Centro, Cuauhtémoc, 06010, CDMC

, ext. 6137, 6430

Opening hours: Monday — Friday 9:00 — 18:00

Merida, Yucatan state

Address: Centro Comercial «The Harbor Mérida», Montejo, 97204 Mérida, Yuc.

Opening hours: Monday — Friday 9:00 — 18:00

Monterrey, Nuevo Leon state

Address: Avenida Miguel Alemán, Número 200 (Dentro del inmueble denominado «Paseo La Fe»), Colonia La Fe, 66477, Municipio San Nícolas De Los Garza, Nuevo León

, ext. 6137, 6430

Opening hours: Monday — Friday 9:00 — 18:00

Pachuca de Soto, Hidalgo State

Address: Via Dorada, Av Ferrocarril Central 121, Local 5, Nivel Motor Lobby, Zona Plateada, 42080 Pachuca de Soto, Hgo.

Opening hours: Monday — Friday 9:00 — 18:00

Tijuana, Baja California

Address: Tijuana Airport, Planta Baja del Estacionamiento del Aeropuerto Internacional de Tijuana ubicado en Carretera Aeropuerto s/n, Colonia Nueva Tijuana, C.P. 22435 Tijuana, Baja California

Opening hours: Monday — Friday 9:00 — 18:00
