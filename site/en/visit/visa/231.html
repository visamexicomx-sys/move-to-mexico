Getting a US Visa in Mexico - Passport and Visas

Purchasing a US visa — the process is long and painstaking. Contrary to popular belief, applying for an American visa — This is not a lottery, but the result of an analysis of the applicant’s data by a visa officer. It is important to take into account all the details when preparing papers, since any little thing can influence his decision. A special feature of most types of American visas in Mexico is that you must be at least a resident of this country. But the steps at American consulates in Mexico are not much different from procedures in other countries.

In Mexico, visas are issued by the consular section of the US Embassy in Mexico City and 9 full-fledged consulates in different states. The closest American consulate to Cancun is located in Merida, Yucatan. Consular Section in Mexico City — the busiest, therefore there are the longest lines. Before visiting the consulate, you must register and visit the ASC (Applicant Service Center, applicant service centers), where you will have to be fingerprinted and have your photograph taken. If you already have a US visa, most often you will not have to go through an interview at the consulate.
