Marriage registration in Mexico in one day – Wedding in Mexico

For more than three years now, we have been assisting with the official registration of marriages in Mexico, in particular in Cancun. Quintana Roo — the only state in Mexico where to get married you must have a passport and take a blood test, and nothing else. Simplified criteria do not mean that you are entering into some kind of “under-marriage.” A marriage certificate issued in Quintana Roo is no different from certificates issued in other states of Mexico, and the marriage has the same legal consequences for the newlyweds as marriages in other countries of the world.

Simplified norms for registering marriages in Cancun — the result of the painstaking work of Mexican legislators and the local government establishment to transform Cancun into a key world resort. Simplified conditions not only allow you to avoid unnecessary formalities, but also significantly speed up the entire process. With our assistance, you can register your marriage in 1 day.

Our services are primarily for those who do not want to independently prepare for registration, visit medical laboratories, registry offices and municipal institutions. For those who want to quickly get a marriage certificate upon arriving in Cancun, and then leave or devote the rest of their vacation to a beach holiday. In this regard, you do not go to the hospital to take a blood test, but the laboratory itself comes to your hotel and then sends the test result by email. And this analysis will be ready in a couple of hours, and not the next day. We do not work with registry offices, but directly with judges who register marriages. This opens the way to avoid queues, assign a check-in place and time that is practical for the client, and even carry out check-in on a weekend. You can take a blood test and immediately come to register your marriage at the judge’s office. Or able to take the test in the judge’s office before registering the marriage. Or the judge and assistants may come to your hotel.

By putting your fingerprints on your marriage certificate, you can immediately leave Cancun or Mexico. We ourselves will submit the certificate for apostille and then send it to you by courier to any city in Mexico or any country in the world (to Russia — with restrictions). If you stay in Cancun, we will deliver the apostille certificate to your hotel at a time convenient to you. If you need to translate a marriage certificate, after apostilling we will also organize the translation.

We have optimized all processes as much as possible in order to protect the newlyweds from unnecessary hassle and give them the opportunity to devote more time to relaxation and to each other. We understand perfectly well that a trip to Cancun to register a marriage — This is a great prospect to combine business with pleasure. You'll have more time to relax on the beach or visit the pyramids, cenotes and other attractions along Mexico's Caribbean coast — the most romantic place on Earth.

So, to register a marriage in Cancun, newlyweds should only take their passports with them. No other documentation is needed! Already on the eve you are required to take blood tests and come to register with the judge. It's simple! We talked about thisHere.

Please use our extensive experience in arranging official marriage registration in Mexico. With our support, one day of presence in Cancun is really enough to register a marriage!
