Fast marriage registration in Cancun in just 1 day — Свадьба в Мексике

We offer expedited marriage registration in Cancun, Mexico, with the entire process completed in just one day. This express option is available for foreign nationals, Mexican citizens, and mixed-nationality couples. You meet with the judge, sign the documents, and receive your official marriage certificate the same day — quickly, legally, and without unnecessary formalities.

There is no need to:

  • Wait for an available registration date
  • Obtain civil registry extracts in advance
  • Take blood tests
  • Have your passport officially translated into Spanish
  • Provide a birth certificate or certificate of single status
  • Bring your own witnesses

What you need to do:

  • Agree on the date and time with the judge in advance
  • Send scanned copies of your passports so we can prepare all required documents

Price: 1,900 USD — All Inclusive

Simply let us know your arrival date in Mexico and send passport copies — that is all we need to arrange your marriage registration. We strictly adhere to the agreed timelines and conditions.

We can also legalize your marriage certificate with an apostille for official use abroad. Apostille processing is included in the price. The apostille is issued within 3 business days and cannot be expedited. However, we can arrange courier delivery of the apostilled marriage certificate within Mexico or internationally.

If you prefer the standard (non-express) marriage registration procedure, please see this option.
