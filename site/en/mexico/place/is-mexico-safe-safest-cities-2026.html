Is Mexico Safe? The Safest Cities in 2026

"Is Mexico safe?" is the number-one question for anyone planning a move. The answer depends on the region: Mexico is huge, and safety in tourist and expat zones is dramatically different from what the news shows. Here's the real picture for 2026.

Safest states and cities

PlaceSafety level
Merida (Yucatan)One of the safest cities in Latin America
Yucatan stateLowest crime rate in the country
Quintana Roo (Playa, Tulum)Tourist and residential zones are safe
San Miguel de AllendeCalm colonial town
CampecheQuiet, underrated, very safe

The real picture

Safety issues in Mexico are concentrated in specific regions tied to drug trafficking and barely touch tourists and expats. Yucatan is statistically safer than many US states. In Merida people walk around in the evening and petty crime is minimal.

Safety tips for expats

  • Choose reputable neighborhoods — as in any country.
  • Use Uber instead of street taxis.
  • Don't flash expensive items or large amounts of cash.
  • Avoid night drives on highways between distant regions.
  • Follow official advisories for specific states.

Bottom line

For expat living, Mexico's safe regions — Yucatan, Quintana Roo, the colonial towns — offer a calm comparable to Europe. The key is the right choice of city and neighborhood.
