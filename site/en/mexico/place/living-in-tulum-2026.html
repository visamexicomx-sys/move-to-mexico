Living in Tulum in 2026

Tulum is the trendiest, most eco-minded town on the Riviera Maya: white beaches, a bohemian vibe, yoga and design hotels. But living here has its nuances. Here is living in Tulum in 2026.

Pros and cons

ProsCons
Stunning beaches and cenotesPricier than Playa and Cancun
Bohemian eco atmosphereWeaker infrastructure (water, power)
Networker and creative communityThe beach zone is very expensive
The Maya Train improved transportSeasonal sargassum

Cost of living

  • Studio rent in the center (Tulum Pueblo): $600–$900, near the beach much more.
  • Comfortable single lifestyle: $1,800–$2,800/month.
  • Restaurants and services in the tourist zone cost above Mexico average.

Where to live

Tulum Pueblo (the center) is more affordable and convenient for daily life. The beach zone (Zona Hotelera) is expensive and more for leisure. New residential complexes are rising in Aldea Zama and La Veleta.

Who Tulum is for

Ideal for those who value nature, an eco lifestyle and a creative community, and can accept pricier, less developed infrastructure. Families with children more often choose Playa del Carmen or Merida.
