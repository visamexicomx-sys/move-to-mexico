Mexican phone number for receiving SMS and registering on social networks - How to live in Mexico

Sometimes you need an additional active phone number to register or confirm another account on social networks or instant messengers. In fact, the main function of such a phone number — receive SMS on it. In this regard, I would like the costs of servicing this room to be minimal. If you are in Mexico, the best solution is to purchase a number from a mobile operatorUnefon.

Unefon — a full-fledged cellular operator, a subsidiary of AT&T. It has fairly low rates and nationwide coverage.

Unefon's cheapest prepaid package costs 50 Mexican pesos. It has the same amount on its balance, and it will be enough for 5 days of using the phone (mobile Internet + calls and SMS). There is no need to activate the package — You just insert the SIM card into your phone and use it.

Our task — The number for receiving SMS should be active for as long as possible. This chance will remain for 3 months after the number balance is reset to zero. To prevent your number from being completely deactivated, you must remember to top up your balance with at least 10 MXN at least once every 3 months. You can top up your balance by 10 pesos through the Unefon mobile application or by at least 40 pesos in convenience stores. OXXO, at supermarket checkouts or in crowded places.

Unefon's prepaid sales points are not as widespread as those of other operators. They are in shopping centers, Coppel stores and other places (signs with black Unefon lettering on a yellow background).

Sometimes Unefon has promotions where they give away SIM cards with a zero balance. Such a SIM card must first be activated by phone (*100 call), and then topped up with any amount.
