Internet, SIM and Remote Work in Mexico in 2026

For remote work, connectivity and internet are critical. The good news: in Mexican cities the internet is fast and affordable. Here is connectivity and remote work in 2026.

Mobile and SIM

CarrierNotes
TelcelBest nationwide coverage, pricier
AT&T MexicoGood city coverage, cheaper
MovistarBudget option

A SIM costs $5–$10 and a data plan from $10/month, issued with a passport and no contract. eSIMs are available for a quick start before arrival.

Home internet

In cities (Cancun, Playa, Merida, Mexico City) fiber from Telmex/Totalplay/izzi delivers 100–500 Mbps for $25–$50/month. For rentals, confirm whether internet is connected, or set it up yourself (needs an address and sometimes residency).

Coworking

Playa del Carmen, Tulum, Merida and Mexico City have plenty of coworking spaces ($80–$200/month) and cafes with fast Wi-Fi, plus large digital-nomad communities.

Tips for remote workers

  • Keep backup mobile data for power outages.
  • Mexico time zone is convenient for working with the US.
  • To formalize income, get an RFC and the RESICO regime — see our business and taxes section.
